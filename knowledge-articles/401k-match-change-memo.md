# Memo: 401(k) Match Increase Effective October 1, 2026

To: All Tamarack Outdoor employees
From: Marguerite Ellison, Chief People Officer
Date: August 14, 2026
Subject: Tamarack 401(k) match increase effective October 1, 2026

## The Tamarack 401(k) match is increasing

Good news: starting October 1, 2026, Tamarack is increasing its 401(k) match so that more of your retirement savings comes from Tamarack.

The new Tamarack 401(k) match is 100% of the first 3% of pay you contribute, plus 50% of the next 2% of pay you contribute. The maximum Tamarack match is now 4% of your pay.

## The previous 401(k) match

Through September 30, 2026, the Tamarack 401(k) match is 50% of the first 6% of pay you contribute, with a maximum match of 3% of pay. That match applies to paychecks dated through September 30, 2026.

## When the new 401(k) match starts

The new 401(k) match applies to paychecks dated on or after October 1, 2026. The first paycheck with the new match is Friday, October 2, 2026.

## Example: 401(k) match at a 5% contribution

Here's how the 401(k) match change works for an employee who contributes 5% of pay:

- Previous match (through September 30, 2026): 50% of 5% = 2.5% of pay.
- New match (from October 1, 2026): 100% of the first 3% (3%) plus 50% of the next 2% (1%) = 4% of pay.

On a paycheck of $2,000, that's a Tamarack match of $50 under the previous match and $80 under the new match.

Employees who contribute at least 5% of pay receive the full 4% match. Employees who contribute 3% receive a 3% match, up from 1.5%.

## 401(k) vesting is unchanged

401(k) vesting does not change with the new match. The Tamarack match vests 25% per completed year of service, and you are fully vested at 4 years. Your own contributions are always 100% vested.

## 401(k) eligibility is unchanged

401(k) eligibility does not change. Full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment, part-time employees after 1,000 hours worked in a 12-month period, and seasonal employees are not eligible.

## What you need to do about the 401(k) match change

You don't need to do anything to receive the new 401(k) match. It applies automatically to your contributions starting with paychecks dated October 1, 2026.

If you'd like to review your contribution percentage, you can change it any pay period in Tamarack HR Hub under Retirement. HR cannot give investment advice about your 401(k) choices.

## Questions about the 401(k) match change

For questions about the 401(k) match change, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example. Thank you for everything you do for Tamarack.
