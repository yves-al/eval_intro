# Workplace Accommodation Requests

Tamarack Outdoor provides workplace accommodations so everyone can do their best work. This policy explains how to ask for an accommodation and what happens next. It applies to all employees and job applicants and is effective January 1, 2026.

## What a workplace accommodation is

A workplace accommodation is a change to your job, schedule or work environment that helps you do your job. Tamarack considers accommodation requests related to:

- A disability or medical condition
- Pregnancy, childbirth or a related condition, including lactation
- A sincerely held religious belief or practice

## Examples of workplace accommodations

Examples of accommodations Tamarack has provided include:

- A stool or seat at a store register, or a modified lifting limit
- Schedule changes for medical appointments or treatment
- Extra breaks and a private space for lactation
- Temporary reassignment of heavy stock work during pregnancy
- Schedule changes or shift swaps for religious observance
- Exceptions to the dress code for religious dress or grooming
- Assistive technology or ergonomic equipment

## How to request a workplace accommodation

You can request a workplace accommodation in any of these ways:

- Open an accommodation case in Tamarack HR Hub
- Tell your manager or your HR partner
- Ask the HR assistant, which can open an accommodation case for you
- Email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example

You don't need to use special words or fill out a form first. Saying you need a change at work because of a medical condition, pregnancy or religious practice is enough to start the process.

## Your HR partner owns your accommodation request

Every accommodation request is owned by an HR partner, not your manager. Your HR partner contacts you within 2 business days of receiving the request, works with you through the process and makes the decision. If your manager hears your request first, they pass it to HR.

## The interactive process for accommodations

The interactive process is how Tamarack and you work out an accommodation together. Your HR partner will:

1. Talk with you about your needs and the parts of your job affected.
2. Ask for supporting information when needed, such as a note from your health care provider about your limitations (not your diagnosis).
3. Consider options with you and, where needed, your manager.
4. Tell you the decision in writing and put the accommodation in place.

If the accommodation you asked for isn't possible, your HR partner will work with you on an effective alternative. Accommodations can be reviewed if your needs change.

## Accommodation confidentiality

Medical information you share for an accommodation request is kept confidential and stored separately from your personnel file. Your manager is told only what they need to put the accommodation in place, such as a schedule change, not your diagnosis or medical details.

## No retaliation for accommodation requests

Tamarack prohibits retaliation against anyone who requests an accommodation or helps with the process. If you believe you've been treated unfairly, contact your HR partner, the ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142 or ethics@tamarack-outdoor.example.

## Accommodations and leave

Sometimes the right accommodation is time off, such as a reduced schedule or a short leave. Your HR partner can coordinate that with Tamarack Family and Medical Leave and any state-administered programs that apply.
