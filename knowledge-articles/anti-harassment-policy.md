# Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination Policy

This policy applies to all Tamarack Outdoor employees, managers, applicants, contractors, vendors and customers in every Tamarack location and while working remotely. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Tamarack's commitment against harassment and discrimination

Tamarack is committed to a workplace where everyone is treated with respect. Tamarack prohibits harassment and discrimination by or against anyone in a Tamarack store, the distribution center, HQ, at company events, while traveling for work, and in messages, calls and social media connected to work. This policy covers conduct by coworkers, managers, customers and vendors.

## Protected characteristics under Tamarack policy

Tamarack policy prohibits treating anyone differently or harassing them because of race, color, religion, creed, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national origin, ancestry, age, disability, genetic information, military or veteran status, marital status, or any other characteristic protected under Tamarack policy. Employment decisions at Tamarack, including hiring, scheduling, pay, promotion and discipline, are based on job-related factors only.

## Prohibited harassment and discriminatory conduct

Prohibited conduct under the Tamarack anti-harassment policy includes:

- Slurs, jokes, insults or name-calling tied to a protected characteristic.
- Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or sexual comments, images or touching.
- Threats, intimidation or physical contact meant to humiliate.
- Displaying or sending offensive images, messages or objects.
- Making a job decision, a schedule or a benefit depend on accepting unwelcome conduct.

A single serious incident can violate this policy. Conduct does not have to be illegal to violate Tamarack policy.

## How to report harassment or discrimination

Employees can report harassment or discrimination to any manager, an HR partner, the Tamarack ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142, or by email to ethics@tamarack-outdoor.example. An employee does not have to report to their own manager and can skip anyone involved in the conduct. Witnesses are encouraged to report too. Managers who receive a harassment or discrimination report, or who see it happen, must notify HR within 1 business day, even if the employee asks them not to.

## How Tamarack investigates reports

Tamarack treats every harassment or discrimination report as urgent. An HR partner or the Employee Relations team reviews the report, talks with the person who reported, the people involved and any witnesses, and looks at relevant records. Tamarack may take interim steps during the review, such as schedule changes, to protect the people involved. When the review is finished, the employee who reported is told that it is closed and, where appropriate, that action was taken. Employees who violate this policy face discipline up to and including termination.

## Confidentiality of harassment reports

Tamarack keeps harassment and discrimination reports confidential to the extent possible while still doing a fair review. Information is shared only with people who need it to investigate or to take action. Employees who take part in a review are asked not to discuss it with coworkers so the review stays fair.

## No retaliation for reporting

Retaliation is prohibited. Tamarack does not allow anyone to be punished, demoted, have hours cut, be excluded or be treated worse for reporting harassment or discrimination in good faith, for helping with a review, or for asking for an accommodation. Retaliation is itself a serious violation of Tamarack policy and should be reported through the same channels: any manager, an HR partner, the ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142 or ethics@tamarack-outdoor.example.
