# Basecamp PPO Plan Summary

This summary explains what you pay and how coverage works in the Basecamp PPO, one of Tamarack Outdoor's 2026 medical plans. Basecamp PPO is offered to benefits-eligible employees in all states, effective January 1, 2026.

## Basecamp PPO at a glance

Basecamp PPO is a preferred provider organization plan. You can see any provider without a referral, and you pay less when you use in-network providers.

- Deductible: $750 individual, $1,500 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $3,500 individual, $7,000 family
- Health savings account: not available with Basecamp PPO

Once you reach the out-of-pocket maximum for the year, Basecamp PPO pays 100% of covered in-network care for the rest of the calendar year.

## Basecamp PPO cost per paycheck

Basecamp PPO employee costs in 2026, per paycheck (26 paychecks per year) and per year:

- Employee only: $62.00 per paycheck ($1,612 per year)
- Employee + spouse or domestic partner: $148.00 per paycheck ($3,848 per year)
- Employee + child(ren): $126.00 per paycheck ($3,276 per year)
- Family: $214.00 per paycheck ($5,564 per year)

## Basecamp PPO copays

Basecamp PPO in-network copays in 2026:

- Primary care visit: $25 copay
- Specialist visit: $50 copay
- Urgent care visit: $75 copay
- Emergency room visit: $300 copay after the deductible

Primary care, specialist and urgent care copays apply before you meet the deductible, so you pay only the copay for those visits. For an emergency room visit, you pay the cost until you meet the deductible, then a $300 copay per visit.

## Basecamp PPO coinsurance

For covered Basecamp PPO services that don't have a copay, such as lab work, imaging, outpatient surgery and hospital stays, you pay the full cost until you meet the deductible. After the deductible, you pay 20% coinsurance for in-network care and 40% coinsurance for out-of-network care, until you reach the out-of-pocket maximum.

## Preventive care in Basecamp PPO

In-network preventive care is covered at 100% in Basecamp PPO, with no deductible and no copay. This includes your annual physical, routine immunizations and recommended screenings.

## Basecamp PPO network

Basecamp PPO uses a national provider network, which works well for employees and family members who live in different states. You can see out-of-network providers, but you pay more and may be billed for amounts above what the plan allows. Find in-network providers through the Benefits section of Tamarack HR Hub.

## Enrolling in Basecamp PPO

Basecamp PPO is available to employees with a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week. New employees can enroll within their first 30 days, with coverage starting on the first of the month after 30 days of employment. After that, you can change plans during open enrollment or after a qualifying life event. Questions can go to hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
