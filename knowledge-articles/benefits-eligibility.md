# Who Is Eligible for Which Benefits

This page summarizes which Tamarack Outdoor benefits each employment type can receive. It applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026. Each benefit's own policy has the full details.

## Tamarack employment types

Tamarack has four employment types, and benefit eligibility depends on yours:

- Salaried full-time: salaried, exempt, 40 hours per week
- Hourly full-time: a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week
- Hourly part-time: a standard schedule under 30 hours per week
- Seasonal: temporary employment of up to 5 months, for the holiday season or summer

Seasonal employees are never treated as full-time for holiday, PTO or benefits purposes, whatever hours they work. A seasonal employee scheduled for 30 or more hours per week is still seasonal.

You can see your employment type in your profile in Tamarack HR Hub.

## Medical, dental and vision eligibility by employment type

Medical, dental and vision coverage is available to salaried full-time and hourly full-time employees. Coverage starts on the first of the month after 30 days of employment. Hourly part-time and seasonal employees are not eligible for medical, dental or vision coverage, whatever hours a seasonal employee works.

## PTO eligibility by employment type

PTO is available to salaried full-time, hourly full-time and hourly part-time employees. Part-time employees accrue PTO at part-time rates. PTO accrues from the first pay period and can be used after 60 days of employment. Seasonal employees do not receive PTO, whatever hours they work.

## Sick time eligibility by employment type

Paid sick time is available to every employment type, including part-time and seasonal employees. Hourly employees (full-time, part-time and seasonal) accrue sick time as they work. Salaried employees receive a front-loaded amount each January 1. Amounts depend on your policy state's supplement.

## Holiday eligibility by employment type

Holiday pay depends on your employment type:

- Full-time employees (salaried and hourly) are paid for company holidays when their location is closed, and get 1 floating holiday per calendar year after 90 days of employment.
- Part-time employees are paid for a closed holiday only if it falls on a scheduled shift, and do not get a floating holiday.
- Seasonal employees are not paid for holidays they do not work and do not get a floating holiday, whatever hours they work.

Hourly store employees of any type who work a company holiday receive holiday premium pay of 1.5 times their base rate.

## 401(k) eligibility by employment type

The Tamarack 401(k) Plan is available to full-time employees (salaried and hourly) after 60 days of employment and to part-time employees after they work 1,000 hours in a 12-month period. Seasonal employees are not eligible for the 401(k).

## Parental leave eligibility by employment type

Paid parental leave is available to full-time and part-time employees with 12 months of service. Full-time employees receive 10 weeks at 100% of base pay; part-time employees receive 10 weeks paid at their standard weekly hours. Seasonal employees are not eligible. Births and placements before July 1, 2026 follow the previous policy, under which only full-time employees were eligible.

## Family and medical leave eligibility by employment type

Tamarack Family and Medical Leave is available to any employee, of any employment type, with 12 months of service and at least 1,250 hours worked in the 12 months before the leave starts.

## Tuition reimbursement eligibility by employment type

Tuition reimbursement is available to full-time employees (salaried and hourly) with 1 year of service, up to $3,000 per year. Part-time and seasonal employees are not eligible for tuition reimbursement.

## Employee discount eligibility by employment type

The employee discount is available to every employment type. Salaried full-time, hourly full-time and hourly part-time employees get 40% off regular-priced Tamarack products and 20% off sale items. Seasonal employees get 30% off regular-priced items.

## EAP, bereavement and jury duty eligibility by employment type

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) is available to all employees and household members, including part-time and seasonal employees.

Bereavement leave is available to all employees; it is paid for full-time and part-time employees and unpaid but excused for seasonal employees. Paid jury duty leave of up to 10 days per calendar year is available to all employees.
