# Bereavement Leave

We're sorry for your loss. Tamarack Outdoor provides bereavement leave so you have time to grieve, attend services and take care of family matters. This policy applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## How much bereavement leave you receive

Tamarack provides bereavement leave based on your relationship to the person who died:

- 5 days for the death of a spouse, domestic partner or child (including a stepchild, adopted child or foster child)
- 3 days for the death of another immediate family member

## Who counts as immediate family for bereavement leave

For bereavement leave, immediate family means:

- Parent or stepparent
- Sibling or stepsibling
- Grandparent
- Grandchild
- Parent-in-law (your spouse's or domestic partner's parent)
- Sibling-in-law (your spouse's or domestic partner's sibling, or your sibling's spouse)

A spouse, domestic partner or child is also immediate family and receives 5 days rather than 3. For a loss not on this list, talk with your manager about using PTO.

## How bereavement leave is paid

Bereavement leave is paid at your standard daily hours. Part-time and hourly employees are paid for the shifts they were scheduled to work during the bereavement days.

Seasonal employees receive bereavement leave as unpaid but excused time off. An excused absence does not count against your attendance.

## When to take bereavement leave

Bereavement days do not have to be consecutive. You can take them any time within 60 days of the death, for example to attend a memorial service held a few weeks later. Bereavement days are counted as scheduled workdays, so days you weren't scheduled to work are not used up.

## How to request bereavement leave

Let your manager know as soon as you can, then record bereavement leave in Tamarack HR Hub under Time Off. Tamarack does not usually ask for documentation, though HR may ask for basic information such as an obituary or service notice in some cases.

If you need more time than bereavement leave provides, you can use available PTO, and your manager or HR partner can talk with you about other options.

## Support after a loss

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers free, confidential grief counseling for you and your household members. Call the EAP at 1-800-555-0177. For questions about bereavement leave, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
