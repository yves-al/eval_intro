# California Meal and Rest Breaks

This policy sets out Tamarack Outdoor's meal and rest break rules for hourly employees whose policy state is California, including hourly full-time, hourly part-time and seasonal employees in California stores. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Who the California meal and rest break policy covers

Tamarack's California meal and rest break policy applies to hourly employees who work at a Tamarack location in California. Salaried exempt employees in California plan their own breaks. Store managers in California are responsible for scheduling breaks so every hourly employee on their team can take them. Hourly employees outside California follow their store's or the distribution center's standard break schedule.

## Meal breaks for California employees

For hourly employees in California, Tamarack provides an unpaid, uninterrupted 30-minute meal break that starts before the end of the employee's 5th hour of work. Employees clock out for the meal break and are fully relieved of duty, which means no answering customer questions, phones or radios. Employees who work more than 10 hours in a day get a second unpaid 30-minute meal break that starts before the end of the 10th hour of work.

## Rest breaks for California employees

Tamarack provides hourly employees in California a paid 10-minute rest break for every 4 hours worked. For example, an employee working an 8-hour shift gets two 10-minute rest breaks, one before and one after the meal break when practical. Employees do not clock out for rest breaks. Rest breaks cannot be combined with a meal break or used to start late or leave early.

## Premium pay for a missed break in California

When a Tamarack hourly employee in California misses a meal or rest break, takes it late, or has it cut short, Tamarack pays 1 hour of premium pay at the employee's base rate for each missed break. The premium is added to the paycheck for the pay period in which the break was missed. Employees record a missed, late or short break in Tamarack HR Hub when they clock out for the day, and the manager confirms it.

## Reporting break problems in California

A California employee who is regularly unable to take meal or rest breaks, or who is pressured to skip them, should tell their store manager or an HR partner, or report it through the ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142. Tamarack prohibits retaliation against employees who take their breaks or report break problems. Premium pay that was not paid can be corrected through a payroll case in Tamarack HR Hub.
