# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to every Tamarack Outdoor employee, in stores, at the Fort Worth Distribution Center, at Tamarack HQ and working remotely, including part-time and seasonal employees. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Tamarack values and why the Code of Conduct exists

The Tamarack Code of Conduct describes how employees treat customers, coworkers and the company. Tamarack's values are simple: be honest, look out for each other, take care of the gear and the places we play outside, and own your work. The Code does not cover every situation. When something is not addressed here, employees should ask what an honest, careful person would do, and ask their manager or an HR partner when unsure.

## Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists when an employee's personal interests could affect, or look like they affect, decisions they make for Tamarack. Examples include hiring or supervising a family member or partner, owning part of a supplier or competitor, or doing paid work for a competing outdoor retailer. Employees must disclose a possible conflict of interest as soon as they are aware of it by submitting a conflict of interest disclosure in Tamarack HR Hub. Disclosing a conflict is not a violation; hiding one is. HR and the employee's manager review each disclosure and agree on any steps needed.

## Gifts from vendors and business partners

Tamarack employees may accept a gift from a vendor, supplier or business partner only if it is worth under 50 USD and is not cash, a gift card or a cash equivalent. Gifts worth 50 USD or more must be politely declined or returned, and the employee should tell their manager. Employees must never accept any gift, meal or favor while they are involved in choosing a vendor or negotiating a contract with that vendor. When the value of a gift is unclear, treat it as 50 USD or more.

## Confidential information

Confidential information at Tamarack includes sales figures, pricing plans, product launches before they are announced, vendor terms, and any customer or employee personal information. Employees may share confidential information only with coworkers who need it for their work. Customer and employee personal information is handled under the Employee Information and Privacy policy. The duty to protect confidential information continues after an employee leaves Tamarack.

## Use of company property

Tamarack company property includes store merchandise, demo and rental gear, tools, vehicles, laptops, phones and Tamarack HR Hub accounts. Company property is for Tamarack business. Limited personal use of a laptop or phone is fine when it does not interfere with work. Employees may not take merchandise or demo gear home without a recorded checkout approved by their manager. Damaged, lost or stolen company property should be reported to a manager the same day.

## Social media

Tamarack employees are welcome to share their love of the outdoors on social media. When an employee posts about Tamarack products or the company, they should make clear they are an employee and that the views are their own. Employees may not post confidential information, photos of non-public areas such as stockrooms or the distribution center floor, or customer and coworker personal information. Only the Tamarack communications team speaks for the company publicly.

## Speaking up about Code of Conduct concerns

Employees who see or suspect a violation of the Code of Conduct should speak up. Concerns can go to any manager, an HR partner, the Tamarack ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142 or ethics@tamarack-outdoor.example. The Reporting Concerns and the Ethics Hotline policy explains what happens after a report. Tamarack prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or helps with a review.
