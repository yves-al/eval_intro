# 2026 Dental and Vision Plans

This page covers Tamarack Outdoor's 2026 dental plan (BrightSmile Dental) and vision plan (ClearView Vision): who can enroll, what they cost and what they cover. It is effective January 1, 2026, and the 2026 costs apply through December 31, 2026.

## Who is eligible for dental and vision coverage

Dental and vision eligibility is the same as medical: employees with a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week (salaried full-time and hourly full-time). Part-time and seasonal employees are not eligible for dental or vision coverage. Seasonal employees are never treated as full-time for benefits, whatever hours they work.

Dental and vision coverage starts on the first of the month after 30 days of employment, the same as medical. You can enroll in dental, vision or both without enrolling in a medical plan, and you can choose a different coverage tier for each.

## BrightSmile Dental cost per paycheck

BrightSmile Dental employee costs in 2026, per paycheck (26 paychecks per year) and per year:

- Employee only: $4.50 per paycheck ($117 per year)
- Employee + spouse or domestic partner: $9.00 per paycheck ($234 per year)
- Employee + child(ren): $10.00 per paycheck ($260 per year)
- Family: $15.50 per paycheck ($403 per year)

## What BrightSmile Dental covers

BrightSmile Dental covers, for in-network dentists:

- Preventive care (cleanings, exams and routine X-rays): 100%, with no deductible
- Basic services (fillings and simple extractions): 80% after a $50 deductible
- Major services (crowns, bridges and dentures): 50% after the deductible

BrightSmile Dental pays up to $1,500 per covered person per calendar year.

## ClearView Vision cost per paycheck

ClearView Vision employee costs in 2026, per paycheck (26 paychecks per year) and per year:

- Employee only: $1.80 per paycheck ($46.80 per year)
- Employee + spouse or domestic partner: $3.40 per paycheck ($88.40 per year)
- Employee + child(ren): $3.60 per paycheck ($93.60 per year)
- Family: $5.30 per paycheck ($137.80 per year)

## What ClearView Vision covers

ClearView Vision covers, for in-network providers:

- One eye exam every 12 months, with a $10 copay
- Lenses every 12 months, with a $20 copay
- A $150 allowance toward frames every 24 months, or a $150 allowance toward contact lenses every 12 months instead of glasses

## How annual dental and vision costs are calculated

Tamarack pays biweekly, so there are 26 paychecks per year. The annual cost of dental or vision coverage is the per-paycheck cost times 26. If your coverage starts partway through the year, you pay only for the paychecks while you are enrolled.

## Enrolling in dental and vision

Enroll in dental and vision in the Benefits section of Tamarack HR Hub within your first 30 days. After that, you can make changes during open enrollment (November 2 to November 20, 2026, for 2027 coverage) or after a qualifying life event. Find in-network dentists and eye doctors through Tamarack HR Hub. Questions can go to hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
