# Dress Code

This dress code applies to all Tamarack Outdoor employees in stores, at the Fort Worth Distribution Center and at Tamarack HQ, including part-time and seasonal employees. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Store dress code

Tamarack store employees wear the green Tamarack apron and closed-toe shoes on every shift. Under the apron, store employees wear clean, neat clothing that is safe for lifting and climbing a ladder, such as pants, shorts of at least mid-thigh length, or outdoor-style skirts, with a shirt that covers the shoulders. Clothing with offensive words or images, or with large logos of competing outdoor brands, is not allowed. Tamarack-brand apparel is always welcome.

## Tamarack aprons

Tamarack provides each new store employee with 2 aprons at no cost on their first day. Worn or damaged aprons are replaced free by the store manager. Aprons are Tamarack property and are returned on the employee's last day. Name tags are worn on the apron where customers can see them.

## Closed-toe shoes in stores

Tamarack store employees must wear closed-toe shoes with a non-slip sole while working, including in the stockroom. Sandals, flip-flops, clogs with open backs and slippers are not allowed. Trail runners, hiking shoes and casual sneakers are all fine. Store employees buy their own shoes and may use their employee discount.

## HQ dress code: business casual

Employees at Tamarack HQ in Denver dress business casual. Business casual at Tamarack means clean, neat clothing such as pants, jeans without holes, skirts, dresses, collared or casual shirts, sweaters, and outdoor-style layers. Clean sneakers and hiking shoes are fine. Employees meeting vendors or visitors should dress one step up. Remote employees dress business casual for video meetings with people outside Tamarack.

## Distribution center dress code and safety shoes

Tamarack employees at the Fort Worth Distribution Center must wear safety shoes, meaning shoes with a protective toe and a non-slip sole, and the high-visibility vest Tamarack provides whenever they are on the warehouse floor. Loose clothing, dangling jewelry and open-toe shoes are not allowed on the floor. Long hair is tied back near conveyors.

## Safety shoe reimbursement at the distribution center

Tamarack reimburses distribution center employees up to 50 USD per calendar year for safety shoes. The 50 USD annual safety shoe reimbursement is claimed through an expense report in Tamarack HR Hub with an itemized receipt, submitted within 30 days of purchase. Unused amounts do not carry over to the next year. Distribution center employees can combine the reimbursement with their employee discount when buying safety shoes at a Tamarack store.

## Dress code accommodations

Tamarack provides dress code accommodations for religious dress, hairstyles, disability and pregnancy. Employees who need an accommodation can request one through the Workplace Accommodation Requests process, and an HR partner works with them to find an option that fits.
