# Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

The Tamarack Outdoor Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers free, confidential support for whatever life throws at you, at work or at home. This page applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## What the Employee Assistance Program is

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) is a free, confidential support service provided by Tamarack through an outside EAP provider. EAP counselors and specialists can help with personal, family, work and everyday life challenges, big or small. You don't need to be in crisis to call.

## Who can use the EAP

The EAP is available to all Tamarack employees and their household members. That includes full-time, part-time and seasonal employees. You don't need to be enrolled in a Tamarack medical plan to use the EAP, and there is no waiting period: you can call from your first day.

## EAP cost and confidentiality

The EAP is free. There is no cost to you or your household members for EAP services, including counseling sessions.

The EAP is confidential. The EAP provider does not tell Tamarack who uses the service or what you talk about. Tamarack receives only overall usage numbers with no names. Your manager will not know you called unless you choose to tell them.

## EAP counseling sessions

The EAP provides up to 6 free counseling sessions per issue, for each employee and household member. Sessions can be in person, by video or by phone. If you need more support after 6 sessions, or the issue calls for longer-term care, your EAP counselor can refer you to a provider, and your medical plan may cover that care.

## What the EAP can help with

Examples of what the EAP can help with:

- Stress, anxiety, low mood and burnout
- Grief and loss
- Relationship and family concerns, including parenting
- Alcohol or substance use concerns, for you or someone you care about
- Work concerns, such as conflict with a coworker or a big change at work
- Financial questions, such as budgeting and managing debt
- Finding child care, elder care or other family resources
- Referrals for legal questions, such as wills and landlord issues

## How to reach the EAP

Call the EAP at 1-800-555-0177. The EAP line is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. A counselor will talk with you, understand what you need and set up your sessions or point you to the right resource. You can also find the EAP under Wellbeing in Tamarack HR Hub.

## The EAP in an emergency

The EAP is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. If you're in crisis but not in immediate danger, call the EAP at 1-800-555-0177 any time and a counselor will help right away.

## Questions about the EAP

For questions about the EAP program itself, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example. HR can explain the EAP but does not provide counseling or medical advice; the EAP is the right place for that support.
