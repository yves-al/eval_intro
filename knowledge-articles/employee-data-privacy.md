# Employee Information and Privacy

This policy explains who at Tamarack Outdoor can see which employee information, and how Tamarack protects it. It applies to all employees, managers and HR partners, and is effective January 1, 2026.

## What employee information Tamarack keeps

Tamarack keeps employee records in Tamarack HR Hub. Employee records include job information (job title, location, employment type, standard weekly hours, hire date and manager), schedules, time records, pay, PTO and sick balances, benefit elections, 401(k) contributions, home address and emergency contacts. Medical details, such as doctor's notes, diagnoses, accommodation records and the medical reason for a leave, are kept separately and are visible only to the HR partners who handle them.

## What employees can see about themselves

Every Tamarack employee can see their own records in Tamarack HR Hub, including their job information, schedule, pay stubs, PTO and sick balances, benefit elections and 401(k) contributions. Employees cannot see another employee's records, including a coworker's schedule details beyond the posted team schedule, pay, balances or benefits. Employees who think their own record is wrong can correct personal details under Profile or open a case in Tamarack HR Hub.

## What managers can see about their direct reports

Tamarack managers can see their direct reports' schedules, PTO balances and job information in Tamarack HR Hub. Managers never see an employee's medical details, including diagnoses, doctor's notes, accommodation records or the medical reason for sick time or a leave. Managers can see information only for their own direct reports. A manager cannot see records for employees on other teams, for a peer manager's team, or for people who report to their direct reports.

## What HR partners can see

Tamarack HR partners can see the employee records they need for their work, such as answering a case, handling a leave or accommodation, correcting payroll, or supporting an investigation. HR partners who handle medical information keep it confidential and share it with a manager only as needed to put an approved accommodation or leave in place, without the medical reason.

## The HR assistant follows the same rules

The HR assistant in Tamarack HR Hub follows the same rules as everyone else. It shares an employee's own information with that employee, shares a direct report's schedule, PTO balance and job information with that employee's manager, and does not share another employee's information with someone who is not allowed to see it. The HR assistant does not share medical details with managers. Asking the HR assistant does not give anyone access they would not otherwise have.

## Protecting employee information

Tamarack employees who handle employee or customer information must use it only for their work, keep it in Tamarack systems, and never share it by personal email, text or social media. Employees should not look up a coworker's information out of curiosity, even if a system lets them. Anyone who believes employee information has been seen or shared without permission should report it right away to an HR partner, hr@tamarack-outdoor.example or the ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142.
