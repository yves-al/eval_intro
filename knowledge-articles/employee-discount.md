# Employee Discount

This policy describes the Tamarack Outdoor employee discount for all employees, including part-time and seasonal employees. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Employee discount rates

Tamarack employees receive 40% off regular-priced Tamarack products and 20% off sale items. The employee discount applies to Tamarack products sold in Tamarack stores, including apparel, footwear, camping, climbing, paddling and cycling gear. The employee discount starts on the employee's first day of work. The employee discount cannot be combined with customer coupons.

## Seasonal employee discount

Seasonal employees at Tamarack receive 30% off regular-priced items instead of the 40% regular discount. On sale items, seasonal employees receive the same 20% off sale items as all other employees. The seasonal employee discount is available from the first day of the seasonal assignment through its last day.

## Using the employee discount at the register

To use the Tamarack employee discount, employees show their employee ID at the register in any Tamarack store. Employees may not ring up their own purchases or purchases for family members. A coworker or manager rings up the sale. Returns of discounted items are refunded at the discounted price paid.

## Household member discount

One household member may use the Tamarack employee discount, but only with the employee present at the time of purchase. The employee chooses their household member in Tamarack HR Hub and can change the choice once per calendar year. The household member receives the same discount rate as the employee: 40% off regular-priced items and 20% off sale items for most employees, or 30% off regular-priced items and 20% off sale items for a seasonal employee's household member.

## Discount limits and resale

The Tamarack employee discount is for personal use only. Buying items with the discount for friends outside the household, for a business or for resale is prohibited, including selling items online. Purchases that look like buying for resale, such as many of the same item, may be reviewed. Misusing the employee discount can lead to losing the discount and to discipline up to termination.

## When the employee discount ends

The Tamarack employee discount ends on the employee's last day of employment. For seasonal employees, it ends on the last day of the seasonal assignment. A household member's access to the discount ends at the same time.
