# Employee Referral Program

This policy describes the Tamarack Outdoor Employee Referral Program, which rewards employees who refer people Tamarack hires. It applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## Referral bonus amounts

Tamarack pays a referral bonus when an employee refers someone who is hired. The referral bonus is 500 USD for a hire into a store or distribution center role and 2,000 USD for a hire into an HQ role. The bonus amount depends on the role the new hire fills, not on the role of the employee who made the referral. For example, a store associate who refers someone hired into an HQ marketing role receives 2,000 USD.

## When the referral bonus is paid

The Tamarack referral bonus is paid after the new hire has been employed for 90 days. Both employees, the referring employee and the new hire, must still be employed by Tamarack on day 90. If either employee leaves before day 90, no referral bonus is paid. The bonus is paid in the referring employee's next regular paycheck after day 90 and appears on the pay stub as a referral bonus.

## How to submit a referral

To make a referral, an employee submits the candidate's name, email and the job they are interested in through the Referrals page in Tamarack HR Hub before the candidate applies. The candidate then receives an email with a link to apply. A referral submitted after the candidate has already applied is not eligible for the referral bonus. If two employees refer the same person, the first referral submitted gets credit.

## Who is not eligible for a referral bonus

Some Tamarack employees are not eligible for a referral bonus: HR and recruiting team members, the hiring manager for the role, and anyone in the hiring manager's management chain for that role. Rehiring a former Tamarack employee who left less than 12 months earlier does not earn a referral bonus. Referral bonuses are not paid for referring yourself.

## Questions about a referral

Employees can check the status of their referrals on the Referrals page in Tamarack HR Hub. Questions about a referral bonus that seems late or missing can go to HR at hr@tamarack-outdoor.example or through a payroll case in Tamarack HR Hub.
