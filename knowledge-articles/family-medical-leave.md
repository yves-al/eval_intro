# Tamarack Family and Medical Leave

Tamarack Family and Medical Leave gives eligible employees job-protected time off for serious family and medical needs. This policy applies to employees in every state and is effective January 1, 2026.

## What Tamarack Family and Medical Leave provides

Tamarack Family and Medical Leave provides up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave in a rolling 12-month period. Job-protected means you return to the same job, or an equivalent job with the same pay and schedule type, when your leave ends.

The rolling 12-month period looks back 12 months from the date your leave starts. Any family and medical leave you used in those 12 months counts toward the 12 weeks.

## Reasons you can take family and medical leave

Eligible employees can take Tamarack Family and Medical Leave for:

- Your own serious health condition, including pregnancy and recovery from childbirth
- Caring for a spouse, domestic partner, child or parent with a serious health condition
- Bonding with a new child after a birth, adoption or foster placement

You can take the leave all at once, or intermittently or on a reduced schedule when it is medically needed.

## Who is eligible for family and medical leave

To be eligible for Tamarack Family and Medical Leave, you need both:

- 12 months of service with Tamarack, and
- At least 1,250 hours worked in the 12 months before the leave starts.

Eligibility is checked as of the date your leave starts. If you don't meet both requirements on that date, the leave is not Tamarack Family and Medical Leave, but your HR partner can talk with you about other options.

## How hours worked are counted

For the 1,250-hour requirement, hours worked are taken from Tamarack's timekeeping records for hourly employees. For salaried employees, Tamarack assumes 40 hours per week. The 12 months counted are the 12 months immediately before your first day of leave. For example, an hourly employee with a standard schedule of 24 hours per week who worked that schedule for the full 12 months has 1,248 hours, which is short of 1,250.

## Getting paid during family and medical leave

Tamarack Family and Medical Leave is unpaid, but you may use your available PTO and sick time to be paid during the leave. Paid parental leave, when you are eligible for it, runs at the same time as family and medical leave.

Your medical, dental and vision coverage continues during family and medical leave. Your share of the premiums is deducted from any pay you receive; if you have unpaid weeks, HR will tell you how to pay your share.

## State paid leave programs

Employees in California, Washington, New York and Colorado may also be eligible for state-administered paid leave benefits during family and medical leave. Your HR partner helps you with the claim.

## How to request family and medical leave

Request Tamarack Family and Medical Leave in Tamarack HR Hub under Time Off, then Request a Leave. When the leave is foreseeable, such as a planned surgery or an expected birth, request it at least 30 days ahead. When it isn't foreseeable, request it as soon as you can.

Your HR partner reviews your eligibility, may ask for supporting documentation and confirms your leave dates. Your manager is told your leave dates, not the reason for your leave or your medical details. For questions, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
