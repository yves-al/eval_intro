# 2026 Company Holidays and Floating Holiday

This page lists Tamarack Outdoor's 2026 company holidays and explains how holidays work at HQ, the distribution center and our stores. It applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## 2026 company holiday dates

Tamarack observes 10 paid company holidays in 2026. Is a given day a company holiday? Check this list of every 2026 company holiday date:

- Thursday, January 1, 2026: New Year's Day
- Monday, January 19, 2026: Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Monday, May 25, 2026: Memorial Day
- Friday, June 19, 2026: Juneteenth
- Friday, July 3, 2026: Independence Day (observed)
- Monday, September 7, 2026: Labor Day
- Thursday, November 26, 2026: Thanksgiving Day
- Friday, November 27, 2026: Day after Thanksgiving
- Thursday, December 24, 2026: Christmas Eve
- Friday, December 25, 2026: Christmas Day

## Independence Day observed on July 3

Independence Day falls on Saturday, July 4 in 2026, so Tamarack observes the Independence Day company holiday on Friday, July 3, 2026. Saturday, July 4 is not a separate company holiday.

## First company holiday of 2027

The first company holiday of 2027 is New Year's Day on Friday, January 1, 2027. The full 2027 holiday list is published in Tamarack HR Hub before the end of 2026.

## Holidays at Tamarack HQ and the distribution center

Tamarack HQ in Denver and the Fort Worth Distribution Center are closed on all 10 company holidays. Full-time employees are paid their standard daily hours for company holidays when their location is closed.

## Paydays that fall on a company holiday

When a Tamarack payday falls on a company holiday, pay is issued on the last business day before the holiday. In 2026, two paydays move:

- The Friday, November 27, 2026 payday (Day after Thanksgiving) moves to Wednesday, November 25, 2026, because Thursday, November 26 is Thanksgiving Day.
- The Friday, December 25, 2026 payday (Christmas Day) moves to Wednesday, December 23, 2026, because Thursday, December 24 is Christmas Eve.

## Holidays at Tamarack stores

Tamarack stores close on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day only. Stores are open on every other company holiday, including New Year's Day, Independence Day (observed), the day after Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve.

## Holiday premium pay and substitute days for store employees

Store employees who work a company holiday other than Thanksgiving Day or Christmas Day receive holiday compensation:

- Hourly store employees receive holiday premium pay of 1.5 times their base rate for hours worked on the holiday.
- Salaried store employees receive a substitute day off, to be taken within 30 days of the holiday. Schedule the substitute day with your manager in Tamarack HR Hub.

## Floating holiday

Full-time employees get 1 floating holiday per calendar year once they have completed 90 days of employment. Request your floating holiday in Tamarack HR Hub the same way you request PTO. The floating holiday does not carry over: if you don't use it by December 31, it is lost. Part-time and seasonal employees do not receive a floating holiday. Seasonal employees are never treated as full-time for holidays, whatever hours they work.

## Holidays for part-time employees

Part-time employees are paid for a company holiday when their location is closed only if the holiday falls on a day they were scheduled to work. Part-time store employees who work a company holiday receive holiday premium pay of 1.5 times their base rate, the same as other hourly store employees.

## Holidays for seasonal employees

Seasonal employees are not paid for company holidays they do not work. Seasonal employees are never treated as full-time for holiday purposes, whatever hours they work, so they do not receive full-time holiday pay or a floating holiday. Seasonal store employees who work a company holiday receive holiday premium pay of 1.5 times their base rate for hours worked, the same as other hourly store employees. Questions about holiday pay can go to hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
