# HR Case Triage Guide

This guide is for Tamarack Outdoor HR partners and HR team members only. It sets the category, priority, owner and response time for every HR case opened in Tamarack HR Hub, whether an employee, a manager or the HR assistant opened it. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## How HR case priority and response times work

Every Tamarack HR case has a category and a priority set by its topic. Response time means the time to first contact with the employee by the case owner, counted in business days from when the case is opened. Urgent cases get a response the same business day. High priority cases get a response within 2 business days. Normal priority cases get a response within 5 business days. Low priority cases get a response within 10 business days. Case owners may raise a case's priority but never lower it below the topic's priority.

## Harassment and discrimination cases

HR cases about harassment or discrimination (topic harassment_discrimination) are category employee_relations with priority urgent. The Employee Relations team owns them and responds the same business day. These cases include reports of retaliation. Treat every report as urgent even when the employee describes it as minor, and never route it to the employee's manager if the manager is involved.

## Safety and injury cases

HR cases about a workplace injury, near miss or safety hazard (topic safety_injury) are category safety with priority urgent. The Safety and Risk team owns them, working with the location's HR partner, and responds the same business day. The safety team confirms the injury report was filed in Tamarack HR Hub within 24 hours and starts the workers' compensation claim when needed.

## Accommodation and medical cases

HR cases about a disability, pregnancy or religious accommodation, or other medical matters (topic accommodation_medical) are category accommodation with priority high. The employee's assigned HR partner owns them and responds within 2 business days. Medical details stay in the accommodation case and are never shared with the employee's manager.

## Immigration and work authorization cases

HR cases about I-9 verification, reverification or work authorization (topic immigration_work_authorization) are category immigration with priority high. The HR Immigration and Compliance specialist owns them and responds within 2 business days. Tamarack HR does not give immigration legal advice; the specialist handles process and documents only.

## Payroll and benefits cases

HR cases about a payroll error (topic payroll_error) are category payroll with priority normal; the Payroll team owns them and responds within 5 business days. Confirmed errors over 100 USD get an off-cycle check within 3 business days of confirmation. Unresolved benefits questions (topic benefits_unresolved) are category benefits with priority normal; the Benefits team owns them and responds within 5 business days.

## Other cases

HR cases that do not fit another topic (topic other) are category general with priority low. The HR Service Center owns them and responds within 10 business days. If a general case turns out to involve harassment, safety, an accommodation, immigration, payroll or benefits, the HR Service Center recategorizes it to the matching topic and its priority.

## Category and priority reference table

Tamarack HR case topics map to categories and priorities as follows. harassment_discrimination: employee_relations, urgent, same business day. safety_injury: safety, urgent, same business day. accommodation_medical: accommodation, high, 2 business days. immigration_work_authorization: immigration, high, 2 business days. payroll_error: payroll, normal, 5 business days. benefits_unresolved: benefits, normal, 5 business days. other: general, low, 10 business days.
