# Using Tamarack HR Hub

Tamarack HR Hub is the Tamarack Outdoor employee portal for pay, time off, schedules, benefits, expenses and HR cases. This guide applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026. Tamarack HR Hub is available on any computer and in the Tamarack HR Hub app for phones.

## Signing in to Tamarack HR Hub

Employees sign in to Tamarack HR Hub with their Tamarack employee ID and password, and confirm sign-in with a code sent to their phone. New employees receive sign-in instructions by email before their first day. Employees who are locked out can reset their password from the sign-in page or ask their manager to open a case for them. Never share a Tamarack HR Hub password with anyone, including a manager.

## Pay stubs and tax forms in Tamarack HR Hub

Pay stubs are under Pay, then Pay stubs, in Tamarack HR Hub, and each new pay stub is posted by the morning of payday. The Pay section also holds direct deposit settings, tax withholding forms and year-end tax forms. Tamarack cannot give tax advice about how to fill out withholding forms.

## PTO and sick balances in Tamarack HR Hub

Employees see their PTO balance and sick time balance under Time Off, then Balances, in Tamarack HR Hub. Balances shown are as of the end of the most recent completed pay period, and hours earned in the current pay period are added after it closes. The Balances page also shows hours used this year, hours carried over from last year and upcoming approved time off.

## Requesting time off in Tamarack HR Hub

To request PTO, sick time or a floating holiday, employees go to Time Off, then Request, in Tamarack HR Hub, choose the type and dates, and submit the request to their manager. Requests for 3 or more consecutive workdays need 2 weeks' notice. The request shows as pending until the manager approves or declines it, and the employee receives a notification either way. Leaves of absence, such as family and medical leave or parental leave, are requested under Time Off, then Leave.

## Schedules and timecards in Tamarack HR Hub

Store and distribution center employees see posted schedules under Schedule in Tamarack HR Hub, where they also set availability and request shift swaps. Hourly employees see their recorded time and submit timecard corrections under Timecard.

## Updating your address and personal information

Employees update their home address, phone number, emergency contacts and preferred name under Profile in Tamarack HR Hub. A new home address updates mailing and tax information. Remote employees must tell HR before moving to another state, because their residence state sets which state supplements apply to them; a move is not complete in Tamarack HR Hub until HR approves it.

## Benefits enrollment in Tamarack HR Hub

Benefits elections for medical, dental, vision and the 401(k) are under Benefits in Tamarack HR Hub. New employees enroll there when they become eligible, all eligible employees make changes there during open enrollment, and employees report a qualifying life event there within 30 days of the event.

## Expense reports in Tamarack HR Hub

Expense reports are under Expenses, then New report, in Tamarack HR Hub. Employees add each expense with its date, amount, business purpose and a photo of the receipt where one is required, then submit the report to their manager for approval. Travel bookings through Tamarack HR Hub travel are also under Expenses.

## HR cases in Tamarack HR Hub

Employees open an HR case under Help, then Cases, in Tamarack HR Hub for questions or problems that need HR, such as a payroll error, a benefits issue or an accommodation request. Each case gets a case number, and employees can track its status and reply to HR in the same place.
