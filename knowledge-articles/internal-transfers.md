# Internal Transfers and Promotions

This policy explains how Tamarack Outdoor employees apply for internal roles, transfers to other locations and promotions. It applies to all regular employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## Eligibility to apply for an internal role

Tamarack employees are eligible to apply for internal roles after 6 months in their current role. The 6 months count from the date the employee started their current job, not their original hire date, so an employee who changed roles recently starts a new 6-month count. Employees on a written performance improvement plan are not eligible to apply until the plan is completed. The hiring manager may waive the 6-month requirement with HR approval when a business need exists.

## Finding and applying for internal roles

Open Tamarack roles are posted on the Careers page in Tamarack HR Hub at least 5 business days before external candidates are considered. Employees apply through the Careers page using their Tamarack HR Hub login. Internal candidates are considered alongside external candidates, and hiring decisions are based on skills, performance and fit for the role.

## Telling your manager before applying

Tamarack asks employees to tell their current manager before they apply for an internal role. Managers are expected to support career moves, and an employee will not be treated differently for applying. The hiring manager speaks with the employee's current manager and reviews the most recent performance review before making an offer.

## Promotions within your team

A promotion at Tamarack is a move to a role at a higher level, either on the employee's current team or through an internal application. Promotions on the same team are proposed by the manager and approved by the department leader and HR. Promotions may take effect at any time of year and come with a pay increase set at the time of the promotion, separate from the April 1 merit increase.

## Start dates for internal transfers

When a Tamarack employee accepts an internal role, the current manager and the hiring manager agree on a start date, usually within 2 to 4 weeks. Store transfers between November 20 and December 31 may be delayed until January because of holiday staffing. The employee's hire date, PTO balance, PTO tier and years of service stay the same after a transfer.

## Relocation for internal transfers

Tamarack decides relocation support for internal transfers case by case. Relocation support is not guaranteed and depends on the role, the distance and the business need. Any relocation support is agreed in writing before the employee accepts the internal offer. A transfer to a location in a different state changes the employee's policy state to the new work location's state.

## Seasonal employees and regular roles

Seasonal employees who want a regular role at Tamarack can apply on the Careers page in Tamarack HR Hub at any time during their season. The 6-month rule for internal roles does not apply to seasonal employees applying for regular roles. The Seasonal Employment FAQ describes rehire priority for seasonal employees.
