# Jury Duty and Witness Leave

Tamarack Outdoor supports employees who are called to serve on a jury or appear as a witness. This policy applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## Paid jury duty leave

Tamarack provides full pay for jury duty for up to 10 days per calendar year. Full pay means your base pay for your standard daily hours. Hourly employees are paid for the shifts they were scheduled to work on their jury duty days.

The 10 paid days reset each January 1. Days count whether you serve a full day or are released early.

## Jury duty beyond 10 days

If jury duty lasts longer than 10 days in a calendar year, the additional days are unpaid but excused. You may choose to use available PTO for those days instead. Your job is protected for the full length of your jury service.

## How to request jury duty leave

When you receive a jury summons, bring the summons to your manager as soon as you can so your schedule can be adjusted. Then record jury duty in Tamarack HR Hub under Time Off and upload a copy of the summons.

At the end of your service, upload the proof of attendance the court gives you. This lets payroll confirm your paid jury duty days.

## Returning to work after jury duty

If you are released from jury duty with half or more of your scheduled shift remaining, check in with your manager about returning to work for the rest of the day. Store and distribution center employees should call their manager before coming back mid-shift.

## Witness leave

Employees who are subpoenaed to appear as a witness in a court case receive unpaid but excused time off for the time needed to testify. You may use available PTO to be paid for that time.

If you appear as a witness on Tamarack's behalf, the time is paid at your standard daily hours or scheduled shift.

## Requesting witness leave

Witness leave follows the same steps as jury duty: give your manager a copy of the subpoena as soon as you receive it, and record the time in Tamarack HR Hub.

## Questions about jury duty and witness leave

For questions about jury duty or witness leave, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example. Tamarack HR can explain this company policy but cannot give legal advice about your court obligations.
