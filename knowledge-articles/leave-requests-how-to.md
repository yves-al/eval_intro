# Requesting a Leave of Absence

This guide walks you through requesting a leave of absence at Tamarack Outdoor, from submitting the request to coming back to work. It applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## What counts as a leave of absence

A leave of absence is time away from work beyond ordinary PTO or sick days. At Tamarack, leaves of absence include:

- Tamarack Family and Medical Leave
- Paid parental leave
- Leave needed as a workplace accommodation

Bereavement leave and jury duty are recorded under Time Off in Tamarack HR Hub and don't need a leave of absence request. A PTO request for vacation, even a long one, is not a leave of absence; request it as PTO.

## How to request a leave of absence in Tamarack HR Hub

To request a leave of absence:

1. Sign in to Tamarack HR Hub and open Time Off, then Request a Leave.
2. Choose the leave type, such as family and medical leave or parental leave.
3. Enter your expected first day of leave and expected return date.
4. Choose whether you want to use PTO or sick time to be paid during any unpaid part of the leave.
5. Submit the request. Your HR partner contacts you within 2 business days.

Your manager is notified of your leave dates but not the reason for your leave.

## Notice for a leave of absence

When a leave of absence is foreseeable, such as a planned surgery, an expected birth or a planned adoption, request it at least 30 days ahead. When it isn't foreseeable, request it as soon as you can, and let your manager know you'll be out. A family member can contact HR at hr@tamarack-outdoor.example for you if you are unable to.

## Documentation for a leave of absence

Your HR partner tells you what documentation your leave needs. For leave for your own or a family member's serious health condition, that is usually a certification form from the health care provider, due within 15 calendar days after HR sends it to you. For parental leave, upload documentation of the birth, adoption or placement within 30 days after the event.

Send medical documents to HR through Tamarack HR Hub only, not to your manager.

## Pay and benefits during a leave of absence

Tamarack Family and Medical Leave is unpaid, but you can use available PTO and sick time to be paid. Paid parental leave is paid as described in the Paid Parental Leave Policy. Your medical, dental and vision coverage continues during approved leave.

## State paid leave programs

Employees in California, Washington, New York and Colorado may also be eligible for state-administered paid leave benefits during a leave of absence. Your HR partner helps you with the claim.

## Returning to work after a leave of absence

Confirm your return date in Tamarack HR Hub at least 5 business days before you come back. If your plans change, tell your HR partner as soon as you can.

If you were on leave for your own health condition, upload a return-to-work note from your health care provider before your first day back. If you need any changes to your job when you return, your HR partner can start a workplace accommodation request.

## Who to contact about a leave of absence

For questions about a leave of absence, contact your HR partner through Tamarack HR Hub or email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
