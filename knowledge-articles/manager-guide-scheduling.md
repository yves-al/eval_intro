# Manager Guide: Scheduling and Labor

This guide is for Tamarack Outdoor store managers and Fort Worth Distribution Center managers who build schedules and approve time. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Weekly labor budget for managers

Each Tamarack store and the distribution center has a weekly labor budget in hours, set by the district manager (or the distribution center leader) from the sales or volume forecast. Managers see their labor budget and scheduled hours side by side under Schedule in Tamarack HR Hub. Schedules should stay within the labor budget. Going over the weekly labor budget by more than 5% needs the district manager's approval before the schedule is posted.

## Posting schedules 14 days ahead

Tamarack managers must post store and distribution center schedules in Tamarack HR Hub 14 days ahead. Build schedules from each employee's availability in Tamarack HR Hub. After a schedule is posted, change a shift only when needed, tell the employee directly, and make the change in Tamarack HR Hub so the employee gets a notification. Approve or decline shift swap requests promptly; swaps must be approved at least 24 hours before the earlier shift.

## Approving overtime

Tamarack managers must approve overtime in advance. Hourly employees are paid 1.5 times their base rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. In California, hourly employees are also paid 1.5 times for hours over 8 in a workday and 2 times for hours over 12. Check the overtime warning under Schedule in Tamarack HR Hub before posting. If an employee works overtime without approval, pay every hour worked and coach the employee; never ask anyone to work off the clock or edit time to avoid overtime.

## Minimum rest between closing and opening shifts

Tamarack managers must schedule at least 10 hours between an employee's closing shift and their next opening shift, unless the employee agrees in writing to a shorter break. The written agreement is recorded in Tamarack HR Hub before the shift. Never pressure an employee to agree; an employee may decline without any consequence. Tamarack HR Hub flags shifts that break the 10-hour rule.

## California meal and rest breaks in schedules

Managers of California stores must schedule each hourly employee's 30-minute meal break to start before the end of their 5th hour of work, and allow a paid 10-minute rest break for every 4 hours worked. Each missed break costs 1 hour of premium pay at the employee's base rate, so plan coverage so breaks happen.

## Holiday staffing

Tamarack stores are open on every company holiday except Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Hourly employees who work a company holiday receive 1.5 times their base rate, and salaried employees who work one receive a substitute day off within 30 days. Ask for volunteers for holiday shifts first, then rotate holiday shifts fairly across the team year to year. The distribution center is closed on all company holidays.

## Store peak period from November 20 to December 31

From November 20 through December 31, store managers approve PTO under the store peak period rules in the Manager Guide: Approving PTO and Leave, with at most 1 associate off per shift unless the district manager approves more. Plan holiday schedules early and post them 14 days ahead like any other schedule.
