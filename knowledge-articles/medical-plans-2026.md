# 2026 Medical Plans Overview and Costs

This overview compares Tamarack Outdoor's three 2026 medical plans and what each costs you per paycheck and per year. It applies to benefits-eligible employees and is effective January 1, 2026. The 2026 costs apply through December 31, 2026.

## Who is eligible for Tamarack medical coverage

Tamarack medical coverage is available to employees with a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week: salaried full-time and hourly full-time employees. Part-time employees (under 30 hours per week) and seasonal employees are not eligible for medical, dental or vision coverage. Seasonal employees are never treated as full-time for benefits, whatever hours they work.

## When medical coverage starts

Medical coverage for new employees starts on the first of the month after 30 days of employment. For example, if you start on March 10, your 30th day is April 8, and your coverage starts May 1. Enroll in Tamarack HR Hub within your first 30 days.

## Medical plans offered in 2026

Tamarack offers three medical plans in 2026:

- Basecamp PPO: offered in all states. Deductible $750 individual and $1,500 family; out-of-pocket maximum $3,500 individual and $7,000 family.
- Summit HDHP with HSA: offered in all states. Deductible $1,700 individual and $3,400 family; out-of-pocket maximum $4,000 individual and $8,000 family. Includes a Tamarack contribution to your health savings account.
- Valley HMO: offered only to employees whose policy state is California or Washington. No deductible; out-of-pocket maximum $3,000 individual and $6,000 family.

Each plan has four coverage tiers: employee only, employee plus spouse or domestic partner, employee plus child(ren), and family.

## Basecamp PPO costs in 2026

Basecamp PPO employee costs in 2026, per paycheck and per year:

- Employee only: $62.00 per paycheck ($1,612 per year)
- Employee + spouse or domestic partner: $148.00 per paycheck ($3,848 per year)
- Employee + child(ren): $126.00 per paycheck ($3,276 per year)
- Family: $214.00 per paycheck ($5,564 per year)

## Summit HDHP with HSA costs in 2026

Summit HDHP with HSA employee costs in 2026, per paycheck and per year:

- Employee only: $28.00 per paycheck ($728 per year)
- Employee + spouse or domestic partner: $71.00 per paycheck ($1,846 per year)
- Employee + child(ren): $60.00 per paycheck ($1,560 per year)
- Family: $104.00 per paycheck ($2,704 per year)

Tamarack also contributes to your HSA: $500 per year for employee-only coverage and $1,000 per year for any other tier.

## Valley HMO costs in 2026 (California and Washington only)

Valley HMO employee costs in 2026, per paycheck and per year. Valley HMO is offered only to employees whose policy state is California or Washington.

- Employee only: $45.00 per paycheck ($1,170 per year)
- Employee + spouse or domestic partner: $108.00 per paycheck ($2,808 per year)
- Employee + child(ren): $92.00 per paycheck ($2,392 per year)
- Family: $158.00 per paycheck ($4,108 per year)

## How annual medical costs are calculated

Tamarack pays biweekly, so there are 26 paychecks per year. The annual cost of a medical plan is the per-paycheck cost times 26. Medical premiums are deducted from every paycheck while you are enrolled. If your coverage starts partway through the year, you pay only for the paychecks while you are enrolled.

## Which medical plans you can choose

Your policy state decides which medical plans you can choose. On-site employees use the state of their work location, and remote employees use their residence state. Employees whose policy state is California or Washington can choose any of the three plans. Employees in Colorado, Texas and New York can choose Basecamp PPO or Summit HDHP with HSA.
