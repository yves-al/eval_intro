# New Hire Guide: Your First 90 Days

This guide explains what new Tamarack Outdoor employees can expect in their first 90 days, including when each benefit starts. It applies to all new employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## Your first day at Tamarack

On their first day, new Tamarack employees sign in to Tamarack HR Hub, review and sign policies, set up direct deposit, and bring documents for work authorization. Tamarack completes Form I-9 verification within 3 business days of the employee's start date, as described in the Work Authorization and I-9 Verification policy. Store employees receive 2 Tamarack aprons and a name tag. HQ and remote employees receive their laptop.

## Training for new employees

Tamarack new store employees complete 16 hours of paid onboarding training in their first 2 weeks, covering the register, product knowledge, customer service and store safety. New distribution center employees complete safety and equipment training before working on the warehouse floor. HQ employees attend a half-day HQ orientation in their first week. Every new employee completes safety training in their first week of work.

## When medical, dental and vision coverage starts

For new Tamarack employees with a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week, medical, dental and vision coverage starts on the first of the month after 30 days of employment. The start date counts as day 1. For example, an employee who starts on June 15 completes 30 days on July 14, and coverage starts August 1. Eligible new employees enroll in Tamarack HR Hub within their first 30 days. Part-time and seasonal employees are not eligible for medical, dental or vision.

## When new hires can use PTO

New Tamarack employees in PTO-eligible roles start earning PTO with their first pay period. PTO can be used after 60 days of employment. Seasonal employees do not earn PTO. Sick time follows the Paid Sick Time Policy and the supplement for the employee's policy state.

## When new hires can join the 401(k)

Full-time Tamarack employees can join the Tamarack 401(k) Plan after 60 days of employment. Part-time employees become eligible after working 1,000 hours in a 12-month period. Seasonal employees are not eligible for the 401(k). Employees enroll and choose a contribution percentage under Benefits in Tamarack HR Hub.

## When new hires get the floating holiday

Full-time Tamarack employees get 1 floating holiday per calendar year after 90 days of employment. The floating holiday does not carry over to the next year, so an employee who reaches 90 days late in the year should plan to use it before December 31.

## Benefits available from day one

Some Tamarack benefits start on the first day of work: the employee discount, the Employee Assistance Program for the employee and household members, and the Employee Referral Program.

## Check-ins during the first 90 days

Tamarack managers meet with each new employee at 30, 60 and 90 days to talk about how things are going, answer questions and agree on goals. New employees are eligible to apply for internal roles after 6 months in their role, and tuition reimbursement and parental leave become available after 12 months of service.
