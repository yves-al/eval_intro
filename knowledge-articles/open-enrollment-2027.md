# Open Enrollment for 2027 Benefits: FAQ

Open enrollment is your once-a-year chance to change your Tamarack Outdoor medical, dental and vision elections without a qualifying life event. This FAQ applies to benefits-eligible employees and was published October 15, 2026.

## When is open enrollment for 2027 benefits?

Open enrollment for 2027 benefits runs from Monday, November 2, 2026 through Friday, November 20, 2026. You can make or change elections in Tamarack HR Hub until 11:59 p.m. in your local time zone on November 20, 2026. You can go back and edit your elections as many times as you like until the window closes.

## When do 2027 benefit elections take effect?

Elections you make during open enrollment take effect on January 1, 2027. Your current 2026 elections, and the 2026 costs in the plan summaries, stay in place through December 31, 2026.

## Where can I find 2027 plan costs?

2027 plan costs are published in Tamarack HR Hub during open enrollment, on the open enrollment page. The costs in the 2026 plan summaries and the 2026 medical, dental and vision overviews are 2026 costs; they apply through December 31, 2026, and are not the 2027 costs.

## Who can enroll during open enrollment?

Open enrollment is for employees with a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week (salaried full-time and hourly full-time). Part-time and seasonal employees are not eligible for medical, dental or vision coverage, and seasonal employees are not eligible whatever hours they work. Valley HMO is available only to employees whose policy state is California or Washington.

## What happens if I do nothing during open enrollment?

If you do nothing during open enrollment, your current medical, dental and vision elections roll over to 2027 with the same plans, coverage tiers and covered dependents, at the 2027 costs.

There is one exception: HSA contribution amounts do not roll over. If you are enrolled in Summit HDHP with HSA and want to contribute to your HSA through payroll in 2027, you must re-elect your contribution amount during open enrollment. If you don't, your own 2027 HSA payroll contribution is $0. Tamarack's HSA contribution continues as long as you are enrolled in the Summit HDHP.

## How do I enroll or make changes?

To enroll or make changes during open enrollment:

1. Sign in to Tamarack HR Hub and open Benefits, then Open Enrollment.
2. Review the 2027 plans and costs.
3. Choose your medical, dental and vision plans and coverage tiers.
4. Add or remove dependents, and upload any documents requested for new dependents.
5. If you choose Summit HDHP with HSA, enter your 2027 HSA contribution amount.
6. Review and submit. You'll receive a confirmation statement by email.

## Can I change my benefits after November 20?

After open enrollment closes on November 20, 2026, you can change your benefits only if you have a qualifying life event, such as marriage, a birth or adoption, or loss of other coverage, reported within 30 days. See Qualifying Life Events and Mid-Year Changes.

## Who can help with open enrollment questions?

For open enrollment questions, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example or open a benefits case in Tamarack HR Hub. HR can explain the plans and how enrollment works but cannot give tax or medical advice about which plan to choose.
