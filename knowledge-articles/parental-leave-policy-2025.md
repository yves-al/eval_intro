# Paid Parental Leave Policy (2025)

Tamarack Outdoor provides paid parental leave so new parents can spend time with a new child. This policy applies to full-time employees in every state and is effective January 1, 2025.

## Who is eligible for paid parental leave

Paid parental leave is available to full-time employees with at least 12 months of service on the date of the birth or adoption placement. Full-time means salaried full-time employees and hourly employees with a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week.

Part-time employees and seasonal employees are not eligible for paid parental leave. Seasonal employees are never treated as full-time, whatever hours they work.

## Which events qualify for parental leave

Paid parental leave covers the birth of a child or the placement of a child for adoption. It is available to any parent, regardless of gender. Each birth or adoption placement is one qualifying event, even if more than one child is born or placed at the same time.

## How much paid parental leave you receive

Eligible full-time employees receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% of their base pay for each birth or adoption placement.

## When to take parental leave

Paid parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption placement, in one continuous block of 6 weeks. Leave not used within 12 months of the event is forfeited.

You may use your available PTO to extend your time away after paid parental leave ends, subject to your manager's approval.

## How paid parental leave fits with other leave

Paid parental leave runs at the same time as Tamarack Family and Medical Leave for employees who are eligible for both. Employees in California, Washington, New York and Colorado may also be eligible for state-administered paid leave benefits, and HR helps with the claim.

Your medical, dental, vision and 401(k) elections continue during paid parental leave, and your regular payroll deductions continue.

## How to request paid parental leave

Request paid parental leave in Tamarack HR Hub under Time Off, then Request a Leave. When the leave is foreseeable, submit the request at least 30 days before your first day of leave. For an adoption placement on short notice, submit it as soon as you can.

After the birth or placement, upload documentation within 30 days. Your HR partner confirms your eligibility, your leave dates and your pay.

## Adding a new child to your benefits

A birth or adoption placement is a qualifying life event. To add your new child to your medical, dental or vision coverage, report the event in Tamarack HR Hub within 30 days. For questions, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
