# Paid Parental Leave Policy

Tamarack Outdoor provides paid parental leave so new parents can spend time with a new child. This policy applies to full-time and part-time employees in every state and is effective July 1, 2026, for births, adoptions and foster placements on or after that date.

## Who is eligible for paid parental leave

Paid parental leave is available to full-time and part-time employees with at least 12 months of service on the date of the birth, adoption or foster placement. Seasonal employees are not eligible for paid parental leave, whatever hours they work.

Paid parental leave is available to any parent, regardless of gender or how the child joins the family. It covers the birth of a child, the adoption of a child and the placement of a foster child.

## How much paid parental leave you receive

Eligible employees receive 10 weeks of paid parental leave for each birth, adoption or foster placement:

- Full-time employees are paid 100% of their base pay for 10 weeks.
- Part-time employees are paid for their standard weekly hours at their base rate for 10 weeks. For example, a part-time employee with a standard schedule of 24 hours per week is paid for 24 hours each week of leave.

## When and how to take parental leave

Paid parental leave must be used within 12 months of the birth, adoption or foster placement. You can take it all at once or in up to 2 blocks, and each block must be at least 2 weeks long. Any paid parental leave not used within 12 months of the event is forfeited.

## Births and placements before July 1, 2026

The transition rule for paid parental leave is based on the date of the birth or placement, not the date the leave starts. Births, adoptions and foster placements before July 1, 2026 follow the previous parental leave policy, even if the leave is taken after July 1, 2026.

Under the previous policy, full-time employees with 12 months of service receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% of base pay. Part-time employees are not eligible under the previous policy.

For example, a full-time employee whose baby was born on June 20, 2026 receives 6 weeks under the previous policy, even if the leave starts in August 2026. A full-time employee whose adoption placement is on or after July 1, 2026 receives 10 weeks under this policy.

## How paid parental leave fits with other leave

Paid parental leave runs at the same time as Tamarack Family and Medical Leave for employees who are eligible for both. Employees in California, Washington, New York and Colorado may also be eligible for state-administered paid leave benefits, and HR helps with the claim.

Your medical, dental, vision and 401(k) elections continue during paid parental leave, and your regular payroll deductions continue.

## How to request paid parental leave

Request paid parental leave in Tamarack HR Hub under Time Off, then Request a Leave. When the leave is foreseeable, submit the request at least 30 days before your first day of leave. For an adoption or foster placement that happens on short notice, submit it as soon as you can.

You'll be asked for the expected date of the birth or placement. After the event, upload documentation of the birth, adoption or placement within 30 days. Your HR partner confirms your eligibility, your leave dates and your pay.

## Adding a new child to your benefits

A birth, adoption or foster placement is a qualifying life event. To add your new child to your medical, dental or vision coverage, report the event in Tamarack HR Hub within 30 days. See Qualifying Life Events and Mid-Year Changes for details, or email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example with questions.
