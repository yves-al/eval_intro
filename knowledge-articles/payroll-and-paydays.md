# Payroll, Paydays and Pay Stubs

This policy explains how and when Tamarack Outdoor employees are paid in 2026, how to read pay stubs and how to report a payroll error. It applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## Pay schedule and pay periods

Tamarack pays all employees biweekly, with 26 pay periods per year. Each pay period is 14 days long and runs from Sunday through the second Saturday. The first 2026 pay period started on Sunday, January 4, 2026. Payday is the Friday after the pay period ends, so each paycheck covers the two weeks that ended six days earlier. For example, pay period 21 ran from Sunday, October 11 through Saturday, October 24, 2026, and was paid on Friday, October 30, 2026.

## Paydays that fall on a company holiday

When a Tamarack payday falls on a company holiday, employees are paid on the last business day before it that is not a company holiday. In 2026 this happens twice. The payday for pay period 23 moves from Friday, November 27 (the day after Thanksgiving) to Wednesday, November 25. The payday for pay period 25 moves from Friday, December 25 (Christmas Day) to Wednesday, December 23, because December 24 is also a company holiday.

## Direct deposit

Tamarack pays employees by direct deposit. Employees set up or change direct deposit in Tamarack HR Hub under Pay, and can split pay between up to 2 bank accounts. Changes made at least 5 business days before a payday apply to that payday; later changes start with the following payday. New employees who have not set up direct deposit receive a paper check mailed to their home address until they do.

## Pay stubs in Tamarack HR Hub

Tamarack pay stubs are available in Tamarack HR Hub under Pay, then Pay stubs, by the morning of each payday. Each pay stub shows the pay period dates, hours worked, regular and overtime pay, holiday premium pay, deductions for benefits and 401(k), taxes withheld, and any stipend or bonus. Pay stubs from earlier pay periods stay available in Tamarack HR Hub. Employees can download or print them at any time.

## Reporting a payroll error

An employee who thinks their pay is wrong, for example missing hours or a wrong rate, should open a payroll case in Tamarack HR Hub with the pay period and what looks wrong. Payroll reviews every payroll case. Confirmed errors of 100 USD or less are corrected in the next regular payroll. For a confirmed error of more than 100 USD, Tamarack issues an off-cycle check within 3 business days of confirming the error instead of waiting for the next payroll. Tamarack cannot give tax advice about withholding.
