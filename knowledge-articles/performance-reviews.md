# Performance Reviews and Merit Increases

This policy describes Tamarack Outdoor's annual performance review and merit increase cycle. It applies to all employees except seasonal employees, and is effective January 1, 2026.

## Annual performance reviews in March

Tamarack holds annual performance reviews in March. Every regular employee, salaried or hourly, full-time or part-time, receives a written review from their manager covering the previous calendar year, followed by a one-on-one review conversation in March. Seasonal employees do not take part in the annual review cycle; they receive end-of-season feedback from their manager instead.

## Self-assessment before the review

Before the March performance review, each Tamarack employee completes a self-assessment in Tamarack HR Hub. The self-assessment opens in February and asks employees to describe their key work from the year, what went well, and what they want to grow in. Managers read the self-assessment before writing the review.

## Performance ratings

Tamarack performance reviews use a four-level rating: Exceeds Expectations, Fully Meets Expectations, Partially Meets Expectations and Does Not Meet Expectations. The rating reflects results and how the employee worked with customers and coworkers, guided by the Tamarack values in the Code of Conduct. Employees can see their final review and rating in Tamarack HR Hub after the review conversation.

## Merit increases effective April 1

Tamarack merit increases take effect on April 1 each year. Merit increases are based on the performance rating and the employee's pay compared with the range for their role. Employees learn their merit increase from their manager during or shortly after the March review. Pay at the new rate applies to all work on and after April 1, so the first paycheck that includes it may be a mix of the old and new rates.

## Merit increase eligibility

To be eligible for a merit increase on April 1, a Tamarack employee must have been hired on or before October 1 of the previous year and must still be employed on April 1. Employees hired after October 1 receive a review in March but are first eligible for a merit increase the following April. Seasonal employees are not eligible for merit increases.

## Performance reviews and internal transfers

Tamarack employees are eligible to apply for internal roles after 6 months in their current role. The most recent performance review is shared with the hiring manager for an internal role. The Internal Transfers and Promotions policy has the full details.

## Questions or disagreements about a review

Employees who disagree with their performance review can add written comments in Tamarack HR Hub within 14 days of the review conversation. The comments become part of the review record. Employees can also ask their HR partner for a conversation with their manager's manager.
