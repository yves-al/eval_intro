# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

This policy covers paid time off for salaried full-time, hourly full-time and hourly part-time employees at Tamarack Outdoor in every state where we operate. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Who is eligible for PTO

Paid time off (PTO) at Tamarack is available to salaried full-time, hourly full-time and hourly part-time employees. Seasonal employees do not accrue or use PTO, whatever hours they work.

For PTO purposes, salaried full-time employees and hourly employees with a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week are full-time. Hourly employees with a standard schedule under 30 hours per week are part-time. Seasonal employees are never treated as full-time or part-time for PTO, even if they are scheduled for 30 or more hours per week.

PTO is for vacation, personal days and other time away. Sick time is a separate benefit under the Paid Sick Time Policy.

## How PTO accrues

PTO accrues every pay period. Tamarack pays biweekly, so there are 26 pay periods per year, and each pay period runs Sunday through the second Saturday. PTO starts accruing with your first pay period.

Your PTO accrual rate depends on two things: whether you are full-time or part-time, and your completed years of service on the first day of each pay period.

## When your PTO accrual rate changes

Your PTO accrual tier is set by your completed years of service on the first day of each pay period. When a hire anniversary moves you into a higher tier, the higher rate starts with the first pay period that begins on or after your anniversary date.

For example, if your 5-year anniversary falls on a Wednesday, the pay period already under way finishes at your old rate. The next pay period, which begins on a Sunday, is the first one at the higher rate. If your anniversary falls on a Sunday that starts a pay period, the higher rate starts with that pay period.

## PTO balance cap

Full-time employees can hold up to 160 hours of PTO. Part-time employees can hold up to 80 hours of PTO.

When your PTO balance reaches the cap, accrual pauses. It resumes once you use PTO and your balance drops below the cap. If a regular accrual would take your balance over the cap, you accrue only enough to reach the cap. Hours that would have accrued while you were at the cap are not added later.

## PTO carryover and forfeiture

Full-time employees can carry up to 80 hours of unused PTO into the new year on January 1. Part-time employees can carry up to 40 hours. Any PTO above the carryover maximum is forfeited at the end of December 31.

For example, a full-time employee with 112 hours on December 31 starts January 1 with 80 hours and forfeits 32 hours. A part-time employee with 55 hours on December 31 starts January 1 with 40 hours and forfeits 15 hours.

## Using PTO as a new hire

New employees accrue PTO from their first pay period, but PTO can be used only after 60 days of employment. Hours you accrue during your first 60 days stay in your balance and become available once you pass 60 days.

## Requesting PTO

Request PTO in Tamarack HR Hub. Requests for 3 or more consecutive workdays need at least 2 weeks' notice. For shorter requests, give your manager as much notice as you can.

Your manager reviews PTO requests within 3 business days. You can request only hours that are in your balance; Tamarack HR Hub does not allow a negative PTO balance.

## PTO during the store peak period

Store employees: PTO requests for dates between November 20 and December 31 may be limited by store staffing. Store managers decide these requests using the manager guide for approving PTO and leave. Employees at Tamarack HQ and the Fort Worth Distribution Center are not affected by the store peak period.

## PTO when you leave Tamarack

Unused PTO is paid out when you leave Tamarack, in all states. The payout is made at your base rate of pay. For final pay timing, see Leaving Tamarack: Final Pay and Benefits. Questions about your balance can go to hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
