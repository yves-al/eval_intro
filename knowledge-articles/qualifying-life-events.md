# Qualifying Life Events and Mid-Year Changes

This page explains when you can change your Tamarack Outdoor medical, dental and vision elections outside open enrollment. It applies to benefits-eligible employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## Benefit changes outside open enrollment

Outside open enrollment, Tamarack medical, dental and vision elections can be changed only after a qualifying life event, and the event must be reported within 30 days. The change you make must be consistent with the event. For example, after a birth you can add the baby and change your coverage tier, and after losing other coverage you can enroll yourself or your dependents.

## What counts as a qualifying life event

Qualifying life events at Tamarack include:

- Marriage or starting a registered domestic partnership
- Divorce, legal separation or the end of a domestic partnership
- Birth of a child
- Adoption or placement of a child for adoption, or a foster placement
- Death of a covered spouse, domestic partner or dependent
- Loss of other health coverage, such as a spouse losing coverage through their job
- A move out of your plan's service area, including moving out of California or Washington while enrolled in Valley HMO
- A change in employment type that makes you eligible or ineligible for benefits, such as moving from part-time to full-time

## The 30-day reporting rule

A qualifying life event must be reported in Tamarack HR Hub within 30 days of the event date. If you report it after 30 days, you will need to wait until the next open enrollment to make the change, unless you have another qualifying life event.

## How to report a qualifying life event

Report a qualifying life event in Tamarack HR Hub under Benefits, then Report a Life Event. Choose the event, enter the event date, make your new elections and upload your documents. HR reviews the request and confirms your changes by email.

## Documents needed for a qualifying life event

Upload documents with your request, or within 14 days after you submit it:

- Marriage: marriage certificate
- Domestic partnership: registration certificate or Tamarack domestic partner affidavit
- Divorce or legal separation: the court decree
- Birth: birth certificate or hospital record of birth
- Adoption or foster placement: placement or adoption paperwork
- Loss of other coverage: a letter from the other plan or employer showing the date coverage ended
- Move: proof of your new address, and update your address in Tamarack HR Hub

## When mid-year benefit changes take effect

For a birth, adoption or foster placement, coverage changes are retroactive to the date of the event, so your new child is covered from day one when you report within 30 days.

For all other qualifying life events, changes take effect on the first of the month after you report the event. Per-paycheck costs change with the first paycheck after your new coverage starts.

## Questions about qualifying life events

For questions about a qualifying life event, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example or open a benefits case in Tamarack HR Hub.
