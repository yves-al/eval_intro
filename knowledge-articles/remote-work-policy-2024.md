# Remote Work Policy

This Remote Work Policy applies to Tamarack Outdoor employees who work remotely, and explains remote eligibility, the remote work stipend and the home office allowance. It is effective March 1, 2024.

## Remote eligibility at Tamarack

Remote eligibility at Tamarack covers HQ roles approved for remote work by the employee's manager and department leader. Store and distribution center roles do not have remote eligibility because the work is on-site. Employees with remote eligibility can work remotely full time once their remote work request is approved in Tamarack HR Hub. Approved remote employees receive the remote work stipend and the home office allowance described in this policy.

## Remote eligibility and where remote employees can live

Tamarack remote employees may live and work remotely from any US state. Remote eligibility does not depend on living near a Tamarack store or HQ. Remote employees who move to a new state keep their remote eligibility, the remote work stipend and their home office allowance, and should update their home address in Tamarack HR Hub before the move.

## Remote work stipend amount

The current Tamarack remote work stipend is 50 USD per month for every approved remote employee. The remote work stipend helps cover home internet, phone and utility costs of working remotely. The remote work stipend is paid in the first paycheck of each month and appears on the pay stub. No receipts are needed for the remote work stipend. The remote work stipend begins the month after remote work is approved.

## Remote work stipend questions

Remote employees often ask how the remote work stipend works. The remote work stipend is 50 USD per month, paid monthly, not as a lump sum. Part-month remote work does not change the remote work stipend amount. The remote work stipend ends the month after an employee returns to on-site work or leaves Tamarack. Questions about a missing remote work stipend go to HR at hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.

## Current remote work stipend and home office allowance at a glance

Current remote work stipend: 50 USD per month, paid in the first paycheck of each month. Current home office allowance: 500 USD, one time, reimbursed with receipts. These are the current remote work stipend and home office allowance amounts for every approved remote employee at Tamarack, wherever in the US they work.

## Home office allowance amount

The Tamarack home office allowance is a one-time 500 USD home office allowance for each approved remote employee. The home office allowance can be used for a desk, office chair, monitor, keyboard, headset, lighting or other home office equipment. The home office allowance is reimbursed through an expense report in Tamarack HR Hub with receipts. The home office allowance is paid once per employee, not every year.

## Home office allowance questions

Remote employees often ask what the home office allowance covers. The 500 USD home office allowance covers home office furniture and equipment, not internet service, which the remote work stipend covers. Unused home office allowance does not carry forward as cash. Submit home office allowance expense reports within 90 days of remote approval. Together, the remote work stipend of 50 USD per month and the one-time 500 USD home office allowance make up Tamarack's remote work support.

## Requesting remote work

To request remote work, an HQ employee confirms remote eligibility with their manager and submits a remote work request in Tamarack HR Hub. After the department leader approves it, the employee's profile shows them as remote, the remote work stipend starts, and the employee can claim the home office allowance.
