# Remote Work Policy

This policy applies to Tamarack Outdoor employees who work remotely and to anyone considering a remote arrangement. It is effective January 1, 2026, and replaces all earlier versions of the remote work policy.

## Which roles can be remote

Remote work at Tamarack is available only for HQ roles that the department leader has approved as remote. Store and Fort Worth Distribution Center roles are on-site roles and are not eligible for remote work. An HQ employee who wants to work remotely should talk with their manager, who brings the request to the department leader. Approved remote arrangements are recorded in Tamarack HR Hub, where the employee's profile shows them as remote with their residence state.

## States where remote employees can live and work

Tamarack remote employees must live and work in one of the five states where Tamarack operates: California, Washington, Colorado, Texas or New York. A remote employee who plans to move must tell HR before the move. A move to another of these five states is updated in Tamarack HR Hub. A move to any other state is not allowed under this policy, and the employee would need to discuss other options with their manager and HR.

## Working temporarily from outside your residence state

A Tamarack remote employee who wants to work from outside their residence state, for example while visiting family, may do so for up to 30 days in a calendar year without approval. Working from outside the residence state for more than 30 days in a calendar year needs HR approval in advance. Requests go through a case in Tamarack HR Hub at least 2 weeks before the travel starts. Days count toward the 30-day limit whether or not they are consecutive.

## Monthly payments for remote employees

Approved remote employees at Tamarack receive a remote work stipend of 75 USD per month to help with internet and phone costs. The 75 USD monthly stipend is added to the first paycheck of each month and appears on the pay stub as a separate line. It starts the month after the remote arrangement is approved and stops the month after the employee returns to on-site work or leaves Tamarack. No receipts are needed for the monthly stipend.

## Equipment and one-time home office allowance

Tamarack provides each approved remote employee with a company laptop and a monitor. In addition, remote employees receive a one-time 300 USD home office allowance for items such as a desk, chair, keyboard or lighting. The 300 USD is reimbursed through an expense report in Tamarack HR Hub with receipts, submitted within 90 days of the remote arrangement starting. The one-time allowance is paid once per employee, not once per year. Laptops and monitors remain Tamarack property and are returned when the employee leaves.

## Policy state for remote employees

Tamarack remote employees follow the state supplements for their residence state, not the state of Tamarack HQ. For example, a remote employee who lives in California follows the California Paid Sick Time Supplement and can choose plans offered in California, even though their manager works at HQ in Denver. Remote employees should keep their home address current in Tamarack HR Hub so the correct state rules apply.

## Expectations for remote work

Tamarack remote employees are expected to be reachable during their team's agreed working hours, keep a safe and private workspace, protect confidential information on home networks, and travel to HQ or other sites when their manager asks. Business travel for remote employees follows the Travel and Expense Policy. A department leader may end a remote arrangement with at least 30 days' notice.
