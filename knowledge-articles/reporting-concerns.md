# Reporting Concerns and the Ethics Hotline

This policy explains how any Tamarack Outdoor employee can raise a concern about conduct, safety, ethics or policy violations, and what happens next. It applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## What concerns can be reported to Tamarack

Tamarack wants to hear about anything that seems wrong, unsafe or dishonest. Examples include harassment or discrimination, retaliation, theft or fraud, falsified time records, safety hazards, conflicts of interest, misuse of customer or employee information, and violations of the Code of Conduct. Employees do not need proof to report a concern. Reporting an honest concern that turns out to be mistaken is never a violation.

## Ways to report a concern

Tamarack employees can report a concern in any of these ways:

- Talk to any manager, not only their own.
- Contact an HR partner or email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
- Call the Tamarack ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142.
- Email ethics@tamarack-outdoor.example.

Injuries and urgent safety hazards should also be reported to a manager right away, as described in the Workplace Safety and Injury Reporting policy.

## The Tamarack ethics hotline and anonymous reports

The Tamarack ethics hotline, 1-800-555-0142, is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week by an independent provider, not by Tamarack employees. Callers can give their name or report anonymously. An anonymous caller receives a report number and a PIN, which they use to call back, answer follow-up questions and hear status updates without giving their name. Reports sent to ethics@tamarack-outdoor.example show the sender's email address, so employees who want to stay anonymous should use the hotline.

## What happens after a concern is reported

Every concern reported to the ethics hotline, the ethics email or HR is acknowledged within 2 business days. Anonymous hotline callers get the acknowledgment through their report number. HR then assigns the concern to the right team. Harassment, discrimination and safety concerns are treated as urgent. The reviewer may contact the employee for more detail. When the review is closed, the employee who reported is told that it is closed. Tamarack may not be able to share the specific outcome, especially when discipline of another employee is involved.

## Confidentiality and protection from retaliation

Tamarack keeps reported concerns confidential to the extent possible and shares details only with the people who need them to review and resolve the concern. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith, or who helps with a review, is prohibited. An employee who believes they have been retaliated against should report it through any of the same channels, including the ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142.

## Following up on a reported concern

Employees who reported a concern by name can follow up with the HR contact named in their acknowledgment, or by emailing hr@tamarack-outdoor.example. Anonymous callers follow up by calling the ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142 with their report number and PIN. If more information comes to light after a report, employees should add it the same way rather than starting a new report.
