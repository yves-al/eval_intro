# Tamarack 401(k) Plan

The Tamarack 401(k) Plan helps you save for retirement with your own contributions and a matching contribution from Tamarack Outdoor. This summary is effective January 1, 2026.

## Who is eligible for the Tamarack 401(k) Plan

Eligibility for the Tamarack 401(k) Plan depends on your employment type:

- Full-time employees (salaried full-time and hourly full-time): eligible after 60 days of employment
- Part-time employees: eligible after working 1,000 hours in a 12-month period
- Seasonal employees: not eligible, whatever hours they work

Tamarack HR Hub shows when you become eligible and sends you an email when enrollment opens for you.

## How to enroll in the 401(k)

Enroll in the Tamarack 401(k) Plan in Tamarack HR Hub under Retirement. Choose the percentage of your pay to contribute each paycheck, in whole percentages from 1% to 50%, and choose your investments. The plan is administered by Pinecrest Retirement Services, and you can also view your account on the Pinecrest website.

Enrolling is your choice. If you don't enroll, you can enroll at any time later.

## Tamarack 401(k) match

Tamarack matches 50% of the first 6% of pay you contribute to the 401(k). The maximum Tamarack match is 3% of your pay.

For example:

- If you contribute 6% of your pay, Tamarack adds 3%.
- If you contribute 4% of your pay, Tamarack adds 2%.
- If you contribute 10% of your pay, Tamarack still adds 3%, the maximum.

The match is deposited into your account each paycheck, along with your own contributions.

## 401(k) vesting

Vesting is how much of your account you keep if you leave Tamarack. Your own contributions to the 401(k) are always 100% vested.

The Tamarack match vests 25% per completed year of service: 25% after 1 year, 50% after 2 years, 75% after 3 years and 100% after 4 years. Once you have 4 completed years of service, you are fully vested in all Tamarack matching contributions.

## Changing your 401(k) contributions

You can change your 401(k) contribution percentage any pay period in Tamarack HR Hub, including stopping or restarting contributions. A change takes effect with the first pay period that begins after you save it.

## 401(k) investments

You choose how your 401(k) account is invested from the plan's fund lineup, and you can change your investments at any time on the Pinecrest website. Tamarack HR cannot give investment advice about your 401(k) choices. For help deciding, consider talking with a financial professional.

## Questions about the 401(k)

For questions about eligibility, enrollment or the match, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example. For questions about your account balance or investments, contact Pinecrest Retirement Services through Tamarack HR Hub.
