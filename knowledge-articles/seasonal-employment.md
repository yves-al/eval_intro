# Seasonal Employment FAQ

This FAQ answers common questions from seasonal employees at Tamarack Outdoor stores and the Fort Worth Distribution Center. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## When are Tamarack's seasons and how long do seasonal jobs last?

Tamarack hires seasonal employees for two seasons: the holiday season, from November through January, and the summer season, from May through August. A seasonal assignment at Tamarack lasts up to 5 months. The end date is in the seasonal offer letter and shown in Tamarack HR Hub. A seasonal assignment cannot be extended beyond 5 months; seasonal employees who want to stay can apply for a regular role.

## Do seasonal employees earn PTO?

Seasonal employees at Tamarack do not earn PTO and have no PTO balance. Seasonal employees also do not receive the floating holiday, which is for full-time employees, and are not paid for company holidays they do not work. A seasonal store employee who works a company holiday receives holiday premium pay of 1.5 times their base rate.

## Do seasonal employees get sick time?

Yes. Seasonal employees at Tamarack earn sick time under the Paid Sick Time Policy and the supplement for their policy state, which is the state of their work location. Seasonal employees are hourly, so they earn 1 hour of sick time for every 30 hours worked, with any annual limit set by their state supplement. For example, a seasonal employee in a California store follows the California Paid Sick Time Supplement.

## What employee discount do seasonal employees get?

Seasonal employees at Tamarack receive 30% off regular-priced items and 20% off sale items, starting on the first day of the assignment and ending on its last day. One household member may use the discount with the seasonal employee present.

## Are seasonal employees eligible for benefits?

Seasonal employees at Tamarack are not eligible for medical, dental or vision coverage, the 401(k), paid parental leave or tuition reimbursement. Seasonal employees and their household members can use the Employee Assistance Program at no cost by calling 1-800-555-0177. Bereavement leave for seasonal employees is unpaid but excused.

## How are seasonal employees paid and scheduled?

Seasonal employees at Tamarack are paid biweekly like all employees, follow the same overtime rules, and get schedules posted 14 days ahead in Tamarack HR Hub. Seasonal employees in California follow the California Meal and Rest Breaks policy.

## Do seasonal employees get rehire priority?

Seasonal employees who complete their assignment in good standing get rehire priority at Tamarack. Rehire priority means they are offered a seasonal role at the same location before outside candidates for the next season, if a role is open, and are interviewed for regular roles they apply to. Seasonal employees can apply for regular roles on the Careers page in Tamarack HR Hub at any time.
