# Leaving Tamarack: Final Pay and Benefits

This policy explains what happens to pay, PTO and benefits when an employee leaves Tamarack Outdoor for any reason. It applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## Giving notice when you resign

Tamarack asks employees who resign to give 2 weeks' notice. Employees give notice to their manager, who records the last day in Tamarack HR Hub. During the notice period employees work their scheduled shifts. PTO requested during the notice period is approved at the manager's discretion. Tamarack may choose to end employment before the notice period ends, and pays the employee through the scheduled notice period when it does.

## When final pay is issued

Tamarack's timing for final pay depends on the employee's policy state. For employees whose policy state is California, Tamarack pays final pay on the employee's last day of work. For employees in Washington, Colorado, Texas and New York, Tamarack pays final pay on the next regular payday after the last day. Final pay is issued by direct deposit to the employee's current account unless the employee asks HR for a check.

## Payout of unused PTO at separation

Tamarack pays out unused PTO at separation in all states. The PTO payout is included in final pay and is paid at the employee's base rate. PTO that has not yet been earned cannot be paid out. Seasonal employees do not earn PTO, so they have no PTO payout. Unused sick time and an unused floating holiday are not paid out.

## When benefits end

Tamarack medical, dental and vision coverage ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends. For example, an employee whose last day is October 9 keeps coverage through October 31. Information about options to continue coverage at the employee's own cost is mailed to the employee's home address after the last day. The employee discount ends on the last day of employment.

## Your 401(k) after you leave Tamarack

Money in the Tamarack 401(k) belongs to the employee to the extent it is vested. Employee contributions are always fully vested. After leaving, a former employee generally has options such as leaving the money in the Tamarack plan, rolling it into another retirement plan or account, or taking a distribution. Tamarack cannot give investment or tax advice about these choices, and recommends talking with a financial or tax advisor.

## Returning Tamarack property

Departing Tamarack employees return all company property by their last day, or within 7 days for remote employees. This includes laptops, monitors, keys, badges, aprons and demo gear. Remote employees receive a prepaid shipping box from Tamarack for their laptop and monitor.

## Other items when leaving Tamarack

Referral bonuses are paid only if the referring employee is still employed on the new hire's day 90. Employees who leave within 12 months of receiving tuition reimbursement must repay it under the Tuition Reimbursement policy. Former employees can view and download pay stubs and year-end tax forms in Tamarack HR Hub for 2 years after their last day. Questions go to hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
