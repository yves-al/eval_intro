# California Paid Sick Time Supplement

This supplement sets Tamarack Outdoor's paid sick time rules for employees whose policy state is California. It is effective January 1, 2026, and overrides the company base Paid Sick Time Policy wherever the two differ.

## Who the California sick time supplement applies to

The California supplement applies to employees whose sick time policy state is California:

- On-site employees who work at a Tamarack location in California, such as the San Francisco, Santa Monica or Truckee stores
- Remote employees who live and work in California, even if their team is based at Tamarack HQ in Denver

It covers salaried, hourly full-time, hourly part-time and seasonal employees.

## How hourly employees in California earn sick time

For employees in California, Tamarack's policy is that hourly employees (full-time, part-time and seasonal) accrue 1 hour of paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, up to 80 hours per calendar year. Accrual starts on the first day of work.

## How salaried employees in California receive sick time

For employees in California, Tamarack's policy is that salaried employees receive 80 hours of paid sick time on January 1 each year.

Salaried employees in California hired during the year receive a prorated front-load on their hire date: 80 hours if hired in the first quarter (January to March), 60 hours if hired in the second quarter (April to June), 40 hours if hired in the third quarter (July to September) and 20 hours if hired in the fourth quarter (October to December).

## California sick time carryover

For employees in California, up to 80 hours of unused paid sick time carry over into the next calendar year. Hours above 80 do not carry over.

## Using sick time in California

Employees in California can use paid sick time for the same reasons as the company base policy: their own illness or care, a family member's illness or care, medical appointments and safe leave. Record sick time in Tamarack HR Hub the same way as everyone else, and tell your manager before your shift whenever you can.

## Meal and rest breaks for California store employees

Store employees in California also follow the California Meal and Rest Breaks policy, which sets Tamarack's meal period, rest break and break premium rules for California. That policy is separate from sick time.

## If you move or change locations

If you move out of California as a remote employee, or transfer to a location outside California, your sick time policy state changes. Update your address or location in Tamarack HR Hub, and the supplement for your new policy state (or the company base policy) applies from that point.

## Questions about the California supplement

Anything not addressed in this supplement follows the company base Paid Sick Time Policy. For questions, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
