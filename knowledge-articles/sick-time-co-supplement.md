# Colorado Paid Sick Time Supplement

This supplement sets Tamarack Outdoor's paid sick time rules for employees whose policy state is Colorado. It is effective January 1, 2026. Colorado amounts match the company base Paid Sick Time Policy, with additional permitted uses.

## Who the Colorado sick time supplement applies to

The Colorado supplement applies to employees whose sick time policy state is Colorado:

- On-site employees who work at a Tamarack location in Colorado, including Tamarack HQ in Denver and stores such as Boulder, Vail and Durango
- Remote employees who live and work in Colorado

It covers salaried, hourly full-time, hourly part-time and seasonal employees.

## How hourly employees in Colorado earn sick time

For employees in Colorado, Tamarack's policy matches the company base: hourly employees (full-time, part-time and seasonal) accrue 1 hour of paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours per calendar year. Accrual starts on the first day of work.

## How salaried employees in Colorado receive sick time

For employees in Colorado, salaried employees receive 48 hours of paid sick time on January 1 each year, the same as the company base.

Salaried employees in Colorado hired during the year receive a prorated front-load on their hire date: 48 hours if hired in the first quarter (January to March), 36 hours if hired in the second quarter (April to June), 24 hours if hired in the third quarter (July to September) and 12 hours if hired in the fourth quarter (October to December).

## Colorado sick time carryover

For employees in Colorado, up to 48 hours of unused paid sick time carry over into the next calendar year. Hours above 48 do not carry over.

## What sick time can be used for in Colorado

Employees in Colorado can use paid sick time for all the reasons in the company base policy: their own illness or care, a family member's illness or care, medical appointments and safe leave.

Employees in Colorado may also use paid sick time when their child's school or place of care is closed, for example a school closure that leaves you needing to stay home with your child.

## Recording sick time in Colorado

Employees in Colorado record sick time in Tamarack HR Hub like everyone else: hourly employees on their timecard, salaried employees under Time Off. Tell your manager before your shift whenever you can. When you use sick time for a school closure, choose "School closure" as the reason in Tamarack HR Hub.

## If you move or change locations

If you move out of Colorado as a remote employee, or transfer to a location outside Colorado, your sick time policy state changes. Update your address or location in Tamarack HR Hub, and the supplement for your new policy state (or the company base policy) applies from that point.

## Questions about the Colorado supplement

Anything not addressed in this supplement follows the company base Paid Sick Time Policy. For questions, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
