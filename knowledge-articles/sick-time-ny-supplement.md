# New York Paid Sick Time Supplement

This supplement sets Tamarack Outdoor's paid sick time rules for employees whose policy state is New York. It is effective January 1, 2026, and overrides the company base Paid Sick Time Policy wherever the two differ.

## Who the New York sick time supplement applies to

The New York supplement applies to employees whose sick time policy state is New York:

- On-site employees who work at a Tamarack location in New York, such as the Manhattan, Brooklyn or Lake Placid stores
- Remote employees who live and work in New York

It covers salaried, hourly full-time, hourly part-time and seasonal employees.

## How hourly employees in New York earn sick time

For employees in New York, Tamarack's policy is that hourly employees (full-time, part-time and seasonal) accrue 1 hour of paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, up to 56 hours per calendar year. Accrual starts on the first day of work.

## How salaried employees in New York receive sick time

For employees in New York, Tamarack's policy is that salaried employees receive 56 hours of paid sick time on January 1 each year.

Salaried employees in New York hired during the year receive a prorated front-load on their hire date: 56 hours if hired in the first quarter (January to March), 42 hours if hired in the second quarter (April to June), 28 hours if hired in the third quarter (July to September) and 14 hours if hired in the fourth quarter (October to December).

## New York sick time carryover and annual use

For employees in New York, up to 56 hours of unused paid sick time carry over into the next calendar year. Hours above 56 do not carry over.

Employees in New York may use up to 56 hours of paid sick time per calendar year, even if carryover makes their balance higher than that.

## Using sick time in New York

Employees in New York can use paid sick time for the same reasons as the company base policy: their own illness or care, a family member's illness or care, medical appointments and safe leave. Record sick time in Tamarack HR Hub, and tell your manager before your shift whenever you can.

## If you move or change locations

If you move out of New York as a remote employee, or transfer to a location outside New York, your sick time policy state changes. Update your address or location in Tamarack HR Hub, and the supplement for your new policy state (or the company base policy) applies from that point.

## Questions about the New York supplement

Anything not addressed in this supplement follows the company base Paid Sick Time Policy. For questions, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
