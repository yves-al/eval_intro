# Paid Sick Time Policy

This is Tamarack Outdoor's company base policy for paid sick time. It applies to all employees, including full-time, part-time and seasonal employees, and is effective January 1, 2026. Employees in some states follow a state supplement instead of parts of this base policy.

## Who earns paid sick time

Paid sick time at Tamarack is available to every employee: salaried full-time, hourly full-time, hourly part-time and seasonal. Sick time is separate from PTO. You can see your sick time balance in Tamarack HR Hub.

How you receive sick time depends on how you are paid. Hourly employees earn it as they work. Salaried employees receive a front-loaded amount at the start of the year.

## How hourly employees earn sick time

Under the company base policy, hourly employees (full-time, part-time and seasonal) accrue 1 hour of paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours per calendar year. Accrual starts on your first day of work, and accrued sick time can be used as soon as it is in your balance.

## How salaried employees receive sick time

Under the company base policy, salaried employees receive 48 hours of paid sick time on January 1 each year.

Salaried employees hired during the year receive a prorated front-load on their hire date, based on the quarter they were hired: 48 hours if hired in the first quarter (January to March), 36 hours if hired in the second quarter (April to June), 24 hours if hired in the third quarter (July to September) and 12 hours if hired in the fourth quarter (October to December).

## Sick time carryover

Under the company base policy, up to 48 hours of unused paid sick time carry over into the next calendar year. Hours above the carryover maximum do not carry over. Unused sick time is not paid out when you leave Tamarack.

## What sick time can be used for

Paid sick time at Tamarack can be used for:

- Your own illness, injury or health care
- Caring for a family member who is ill, injured or needs care
- Medical appointments, including preventive care, for you or a family member
- Safe leave: time off related to domestic violence, sexual assault or stalking affecting you or a family member, such as seeking services, relocating or attending court

## How to record sick time

Sick time starts with a heads-up: tell your manager as soon as you know you will be out, before your shift starts whenever you can. Then record the time in Tamarack HR Hub. Hourly employees record sick hours on their timecard for the pay period. Salaried employees enter the sick day under Time Off.

Tamarack does not require a doctor's note for a sick absence of fewer than 3 consecutive scheduled workdays. Your manager should not ask you about your diagnosis. If you have questions about documentation for a longer absence, contact HR.

## Which state's sick time rules apply to you

Your sick time policy state decides which rules apply to you. On-site employees follow the supplement for the state of their work location. Remote employees follow the supplement for the state they live and work in (their residence state).

For example, a remote employee who lives in Sacramento, California follows the California supplement, even if their team is based at Tamarack HQ in Denver.

## State supplements

Paid sick time supplements exist for California, Washington, Colorado and New York. If your policy state has a supplement, the supplement overrides this base policy wherever they differ, including annual accrual limits, front-load amounts and carryover. Anything the supplement does not address follows this base policy. See your state supplement for the details.

Employees whose policy state is Texas follow this company base policy.

## Questions about sick time

For questions about your sick time balance or how this policy applies to you, check Tamarack HR Hub or email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
