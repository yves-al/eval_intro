# Washington Paid Sick Time Supplement

This supplement sets Tamarack Outdoor's paid sick time rules for employees whose policy state is Washington. It is effective January 1, 2026, and overrides the company base Paid Sick Time Policy wherever the two differ.

## Who the Washington sick time supplement applies to

The Washington supplement applies to employees whose sick time policy state is Washington:

- On-site employees who work at a Tamarack location in Washington, such as the Seattle, Spokane or Leavenworth stores
- Remote employees who live and work in Washington

It covers salaried, hourly full-time, hourly part-time and seasonal employees.

## How hourly employees in Washington earn sick time

For employees in Washington, Tamarack's policy is that hourly employees (full-time, part-time and seasonal) accrue 1 hour of paid sick time for every 30 hours worked. There is no annual accrual cap for hourly employees in Washington: you keep earning sick time for every 30 hours worked throughout the year. Accrual starts on the first day of work.

## How salaried employees in Washington receive sick time

For employees in Washington, Tamarack's policy is that salaried employees receive 56 hours of paid sick time on January 1 each year.

Salaried employees in Washington hired during the year receive a prorated front-load on their hire date: 56 hours if hired in the first quarter (January to March), 42 hours if hired in the second quarter (April to June), 28 hours if hired in the third quarter (July to September) and 14 hours if hired in the fourth quarter (October to December).

## Washington sick time carryover

For employees in Washington, up to 40 hours of unused paid sick time carry over into the next calendar year. Hours above 40 do not carry over. This applies to both hourly and salaried employees.

## Using sick time in Washington

Employees in Washington can use paid sick time for the same reasons as the company base policy: their own illness or care, a family member's illness or care, medical appointments and safe leave. Record sick time in Tamarack HR Hub, and tell your manager before your shift whenever you can.

## If you move or change locations

If you move out of Washington as a remote employee, or transfer to a location outside Washington, your sick time policy state changes. Update your address or location in Tamarack HR Hub, and the supplement for your new policy state (or the company base policy) applies from that point.

## Questions about the Washington supplement

Anything not addressed in this supplement follows the company base Paid Sick Time Policy. For questions, email hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
