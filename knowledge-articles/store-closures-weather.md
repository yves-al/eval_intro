# Store Closures and Inclement Weather

This policy explains what happens to Tamarack Outdoor store employees when a store closes or opens late because of severe weather, a power outage or another emergency. It applies to all store employees and is effective January 1, 2026.

## Who decides to close a store

The district manager decides whether a Tamarack store closes, opens late or closes early because of weather or an emergency, working with the store manager. Employee safety comes first. Stores in mountain locations such as Truckee, Bishop, Breckenridge, Vail and Lake Placid may close more often in winter storms.

## How employees find out about a store closure

When a Tamarack store closes, opens late or closes early, the store manager posts a closure notice in Tamarack HR Hub and employees scheduled that day receive a notification in the Tamarack HR Hub app. Employees who are unsure whether their store is open should check Tamarack HR Hub or call their store's main phone line, which plays a recorded closure message.

## Pay for hourly employees when a store closes

When a Tamarack store closes before or during a scheduled shift, hourly employees scheduled for that shift are paid for the scheduled hours they could not work, up to 4 hours per shift, in addition to any hours they already worked. For example, an hourly employee scheduled for 8 hours whose store closes before the shift is paid for 4 hours. An employee scheduled for a 3-hour shift is paid for 3 hours. Employees who were not scheduled that day are not paid for the closure.

## Pay for salaried employees when a store closes

Salaried Tamarack store employees are paid their regular salary when their store closes because of weather or an emergency. Salaried employees may be asked to help with store reopening tasks when it is safe.

## When the store is open but travel is unsafe

If a Tamarack store stays open but an employee cannot travel to work safely, the employee should tell the manager on duty before the shift starts. The employee may use PTO for the missed time or take it unpaid, and the absence does not count against them. Employees should never put themselves at risk to get to work.
