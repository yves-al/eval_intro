# Store and Distribution Center Scheduling and Shift Swaps

This policy explains how schedules work for Tamarack Outdoor store and Fort Worth Distribution Center employees, including part-time and seasonal employees. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## When store and distribution center schedules are posted

Tamarack store and distribution center schedules are posted in Tamarack HR Hub 14 days ahead. Employees can see their posted shifts under Schedule in Tamarack HR Hub and in the Tamarack HR Hub app, and receive a notification when a new schedule is posted. If a manager changes a posted shift, the employee receives a notification and should confirm the change in Tamarack HR Hub.

## Setting your availability

Tamarack store and distribution center employees enter the days and times they are available to work under Availability in Tamarack HR Hub. Managers build schedules from that availability. Availability changes apply to schedules posted after the change, so a change entered today affects shifts about 14 days or more away. Part-time employees can also enter a preferred number of weekly hours, which managers consider but do not guarantee.

## Shift swaps

Tamarack store and distribution center employees may swap shifts with a coworker. A shift swap needs both employees to agree and a manager to approve it at least 24 hours before the earlier of the two shifts. Employees request swaps under Schedule in Tamarack HR Hub, and the coworker accepts there. A swap is not final until the manager approves it. Until then, the original employee is still responsible for the shift. Swaps that would cause overtime need the manager's overtime approval as well.

## Minimum rest between shifts

Tamarack schedules store and distribution center employees for at least 10 hours between a closing shift and the next opening shift, unless the employee agrees in writing to a shorter break. For example, an employee who closes at 10:00 p.m. should not be scheduled to open before 8:00 a.m. the next day. Written agreement is recorded in Tamarack HR Hub, and an employee can say no without any consequence.

## Calling out of a shift

A Tamarack store or distribution center employee who cannot work a scheduled shift because of illness or an emergency must contact the manager on duty by phone at least 2 hours before the shift starts, or as soon as possible in an emergency. A text or app message alone is not enough unless the manager has agreed to it. Sick time and PTO can be used for the missed shift as described in those policies.

## Store hours on holidays

Tamarack stores close on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day only and are open on all other company holidays. Store employees may be scheduled on those other holidays. Hourly store employees who work a company holiday receive holiday premium pay of 1.5 times their base rate. The Fort Worth Distribution Center is closed on all company holidays.
