# Summit HDHP with HSA Plan Summary

This summary explains what you pay and how coverage works in the Summit HDHP with HSA, one of Tamarack Outdoor's 2026 medical plans. Summit HDHP with HSA is offered to benefits-eligible employees in all states, effective January 1, 2026.

## Summit HDHP with HSA at a glance

Summit HDHP with HSA is a high-deductible health plan paired with a health savings account (HSA). It has the lowest per-paycheck cost of Tamarack's medical plans, and Tamarack contributes to your HSA.

- Deductible: $1,700 individual, $3,400 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $4,000 individual, $8,000 family
- Coinsurance: 20% after the deductible
- Health savings account: yes, with a Tamarack contribution

## Summit HDHP cost per paycheck

Summit HDHP with HSA employee costs in 2026, per paycheck (26 paychecks per year) and per year:

- Employee only: $28.00 per paycheck ($728 per year)
- Employee + spouse or domestic partner: $71.00 per paycheck ($1,846 per year)
- Employee + child(ren): $60.00 per paycheck ($1,560 per year)
- Family: $104.00 per paycheck ($2,704 per year)

## How the Summit HDHP deductible and coinsurance work

In the Summit HDHP, you pay the full cost of covered care, including doctor visits and prescriptions, until you meet the deductible. Preventive care is the exception (see below).

After you meet the deductible, you pay 20% coinsurance for covered in-network care and the plan pays 80%. Once you reach the out-of-pocket maximum, the plan pays 100% of covered in-network care for the rest of the calendar year.

## Preventive care in the Summit HDHP

In-network preventive care is covered at 100% in the Summit HDHP, with no deductible. This includes your annual physical, routine immunizations and recommended screenings.

## Tamarack HSA contribution

Tamarack contributes to the HSA of every employee enrolled in Summit HDHP with HSA:

- Employee only: $500 per year
- Employee + spouse or domestic partner: $1,000 per year
- Employee + child(ren): $1,000 per year
- Family: $1,000 per year

The Tamarack HSA contribution is paid per paycheck, in 26 equal deposits: about $19.23 per paycheck for employee-only coverage and about $38.46 per paycheck for the other tiers. If you enroll partway through the year, you receive the per-paycheck deposit for each paycheck while you are enrolled.

## How the HSA works

The health savings account is an account in your name, held with Trailhead HSA, Tamarack's HSA administrator. Tamarack's contributions and any contributions you choose to make through payroll go into it. You can use HSA funds to pay for eligible health care expenses, including your deductible and coinsurance.

Unused HSA money rolls over from year to year, and the account stays yours if you leave Tamarack. You can set or change your own HSA payroll contribution in Tamarack HR Hub at any time.

Tamarack HR cannot give tax advice about HSAs. For questions about how an HSA affects your taxes, talk with a tax professional.

## Enrolling in the Summit HDHP

Summit HDHP with HSA is available to employees with a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week. New employees can enroll within their first 30 days, with coverage starting on the first of the month after 30 days of employment. After that, you can change plans during open enrollment or after a qualifying life event. Questions can go to hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
