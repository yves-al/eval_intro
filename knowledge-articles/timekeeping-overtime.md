# Timekeeping and Overtime

This policy applies to hourly Tamarack Outdoor employees, including hourly full-time, hourly part-time and seasonal employees, and to the managers who approve their time. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Clocking in and out

Tamarack hourly employees record all time worked by clocking in and out at the store or distribution center time clock, or in the Tamarack HR Hub app when working away from the time clock. Employees clock in when they start work, clock out and back in for meal breaks, and clock out when they finish. Work done before clocking in or after clocking out, such as opening tasks or answering a work call, is not allowed. If it happens, the employee must record it so they are paid for it.

## No rounding of recorded time

Tamarack does not round time punches. Hourly employees are paid for the exact minutes recorded between clocking in and clocking out. An employee who clocks in at 8:57 a.m. is paid from 8:57 a.m., and an employee who clocks out at 5:04 p.m. is paid through 5:04 p.m.

## Missed or incorrect time punches

A Tamarack hourly employee who forgets to clock in or out, or sees a wrong punch, submits a timecard correction in Tamarack HR Hub with the correct time and a short reason. The manager approves the correction. Corrections should be submitted before the end of the pay period so they are included in that paycheck. Corrections found after payday are paid through a payroll case, as described in the Payroll, Paydays and Pay Stubs policy.

## The Tamarack workweek

The Tamarack workweek for overtime runs from 12:00 a.m. Sunday through 11:59 p.m. Saturday. Each 14-day pay period contains two workweeks, and overtime is calculated for each workweek separately. Hours cannot be averaged across the two weeks of a pay period.

## Overtime pay for hourly employees

Tamarack hourly employees are paid 1.5 times their base rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek. For example, an hourly employee who works 44 hours in a workweek is paid 40 hours at their base rate and 4 hours at 1.5 times their base rate. Paid time off, sick time and holiday hours that are not worked do not count toward the 40 hours. Salaried exempt employees are not paid overtime.

## California daily overtime

For hourly employees in California, Tamarack also pays 1.5 times the base rate for hours over 8 in a workday and 2 times the base rate for hours over 12 in a workday, in addition to the weekly rule for hours over 40. For example, a California employee who works 13 hours in one day is paid 8 hours at the base rate, 4 hours at 1.5 times and 1 hour at 2 times. Hours already paid as daily overtime are not counted again toward weekly overtime.

## Manager approval for overtime

Overtime must be approved by a manager in advance. An employee who expects to work past their scheduled shift in a way that would cause overtime should ask their manager before doing so. If an employee works overtime without approval, Tamarack still pays for every hour worked at the correct overtime rate, but working unapproved overtime may lead to coaching from the manager.
