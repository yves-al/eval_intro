# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy applies to all Tamarack Outdoor employees who travel or spend money on Tamarack business, including remote employees traveling to HQ. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Booking business travel and flights

Tamarack business flights are booked through Tamarack HR Hub travel at least 14 days ahead when possible. All employees fly economy class. Employees should choose the lowest reasonable fare that fits the business schedule. Travel booked through Tamarack HR Hub travel is billed directly to Tamarack, so the employee does not pay for the flight and does not submit it on an expense report. Business travel needs manager approval before booking.

## Meal per diem for business travel

The Tamarack meal per diem is a daily maximum of 60 USD for meals while traveling overnight on business. In high-cost cities, New York City and San Francisco, the meal per diem is 75 USD per day. Employees are reimbursed for their actual meal costs, including tips, up to the per diem for each travel day. Unused per diem does not carry over to another day. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Meals already provided, such as at a conference, are not claimed.

## Hotel limits per night

Tamarack reimburses hotel stays for business travel up to 220 USD per night, before taxes and fees. In the high-cost cities of New York City and San Francisco, the hotel cap is 350 USD per night. Employees should book standard rooms. A stay above the cap needs written approval from the employee's manager before booking, for example when a conference hotel is the only practical option.

## Mileage reimbursement

Tamarack employees who drive a personal vehicle for business are reimbursed at 0.70 USD per mile. Mileage is claimed for business miles only, such as driving between stores or to a vendor. The employee's normal commute between home and their usual work location is not reimbursed. For example, a 120-mile round trip between two stores is reimbursed at 84 USD. Tolls and business parking are reimbursed separately with receipts.

## Receipts for expenses

Tamarack requires an itemized receipt for any expense of 25 USD or more. Expenses under 25 USD can be submitted without a receipt, but the employee still lists the date, amount and business purpose. Credit card statements are not accepted in place of itemized receipts. Lost receipts for expenses of 25 USD or more need a short written explanation approved by the employee's manager.

## Submitting expense reports

Tamarack expense reports are submitted in Tamarack HR Hub within 30 days of the expense, or within 30 days of returning from a trip. Each report goes to the employee's manager for approval. Approved expenses are reimbursed by direct deposit in a regular paycheck. Expenses submitted more than 30 days late may not be reimbursed.

## Expenses Tamarack does not reimburse

Tamarack does not reimburse personal expenses during business travel, including alcohol, entertainment, upgrades to premium or first class, travel for family members, traffic or parking tickets, minibar charges, or laundry on trips shorter than 5 nights. When unsure whether an expense is covered, employees should ask their manager before spending.
