# Tuition Reimbursement

This policy describes Tamarack Outdoor's tuition reimbursement benefit for eligible employees. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Who is eligible for tuition reimbursement

Tamarack tuition reimbursement is available to full-time employees, meaning salaried full-time and hourly full-time employees, who have completed 1 year of service. Part-time and seasonal employees are not eligible for tuition reimbursement. An employee must have completed 1 year of service before the course starts, and must still be employed by Tamarack when the reimbursement is paid.

## How much tuition reimbursement Tamarack pays

Tamarack reimburses up to 3,000 USD per calendar year for each eligible employee. Tuition, required course fees and required books count toward the 3,000 USD annual maximum. Parking, travel, supplies that the employee keeps, such as laptops, and late fees are not covered. Reimbursement counts toward the calendar year in which it is paid. Unused amounts do not carry over to the next year.

## Which courses qualify for tuition reimbursement

Tamarack tuition reimbursement covers courses from an accredited college, university or recognized certificate program that relate to the employee's current job or to a realistic career path at Tamarack. Examples include retail management, supply chain, accounting, marketing, data analysis and outdoor leadership or wilderness first aid certifications. Courses taken purely for personal interest do not qualify.

## Getting manager approval before the course starts

Tamarack requires manager approval before the course starts. The employee submits a tuition reimbursement request in Tamarack HR Hub with the course name, school, dates and estimated cost, and the manager approves or declines it. Courses that start before manager approval are not eligible, even if the manager would have approved them.

## Grade requirement and submitting for reimbursement

To be reimbursed, the employee must complete the course with a grade of C or better, or a pass in a pass/fail course. Within 30 days of receiving the final grade, the employee submits the grade report and an itemized receipt for tuition and fees through Tamarack HR Hub. Approved reimbursement is paid by direct deposit in a regular paycheck. Courses with a grade below C, a fail or a withdrawal are not reimbursed.

## Repayment if an employee leaves

Employees who leave Tamarack within 12 months of receiving a tuition reimbursement must repay it. The repayment is the full amount reimbursed in the 12 months before the employee's last day. The 12 months are counted separately from each reimbursement payment date. Employees who leave Tamarack and owe a repayment receive a repayment notice from HR and can set up a repayment plan with HR.
