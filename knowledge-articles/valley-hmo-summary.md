# Valley HMO Plan Summary (California and Washington)

This summary explains what you pay and how coverage works in the Valley HMO, one of Tamarack Outdoor's 2026 medical plans. Valley HMO is offered only to benefits-eligible employees whose policy state is California or Washington, effective January 1, 2026.

## Who can enroll in Valley HMO

Valley HMO is offered only to employees whose policy state is California or Washington:

- On-site employees who work at a Tamarack location in California or Washington
- Remote employees whose residence state is California or Washington

You must also be benefits-eligible, with a standard schedule of 30 or more hours per week. Employees whose policy state is Colorado, Texas or New York can choose Basecamp PPO or Summit HDHP with HSA instead.

## Valley HMO at a glance

Valley HMO is a health maintenance organization plan with predictable copays and no deductible.

- Deductible: none ($0 individual, $0 family)
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $3,000 individual, $6,000 family
- Health savings account: not available with Valley HMO

## Valley HMO cost per paycheck

Valley HMO employee costs in 2026, per paycheck (26 paychecks per year) and per year:

- Employee only: $45.00 per paycheck ($1,170 per year)
- Employee + spouse or domestic partner: $108.00 per paycheck ($2,808 per year)
- Employee + child(ren): $92.00 per paycheck ($2,392 per year)
- Family: $158.00 per paycheck ($4,108 per year)

## Valley HMO copays

Valley HMO in-network copays in 2026:

- Primary care visit: $20 copay
- Specialist visit: $40 copay, with a referral from your primary care physician
- Urgent care visit: $50 copay
- Emergency room visit: $250 copay

Because Valley HMO has no deductible, copays apply from your first visit of the year. In-network preventive care is covered at 100% with no copay.

## Valley network and your primary care physician

Valley HMO members must use providers in the Valley network. Each member chooses a primary care physician (PCP) in the Valley network when enrolling; if you don't choose one, Valley assigns one, and you can change it later.

Your PCP coordinates your care. You need a referral from your PCP to see a specialist. Care outside the Valley network is not covered, except for emergencies.

## If you move out of California or Washington

Valley HMO coverage depends on your policy state. If you transfer to a location outside California or Washington, or move out of those states as a remote employee, that is a qualifying life event. Report it in Tamarack HR Hub within 30 days and choose Basecamp PPO or Summit HDHP with HSA.

## Enrolling in Valley HMO

New employees can enroll within their first 30 days, with coverage starting on the first of the month after 30 days of employment. After that, you can change plans during open enrollment or after a qualifying life event. Questions can go to hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.
