# Work Authorization and I-9 Verification

This policy explains Tamarack Outdoor's process for verifying work authorization when employees are hired and when their work authorization documents expire. It applies to all employees and is effective January 1, 2026. It covers process only.

## I-9 verification for new hires

Tamarack completes Form I-9 verification for every new employee within 3 business days of their start date. New employees complete their part of the form in Tamarack HR Hub on or before their first day, then show original documents from the list of acceptable documents to their manager or an HR representative within 3 business days of their start date. Employees choose which acceptable documents to show. Tamarack does not ask for specific documents.

## Reverification before work authorization expires

When an employee's work authorization document has an expiration date, Tamarack reverifies it before it expires. HR sends the employee a reminder 90 days before the expiration date and again 30 days before. The employee shows a current document to HR before the expiration date. Employees should start any renewal early, because an employee who cannot show current work authorization by the expiration date may not be able to keep working until they do.

## Keeping work authorization information private

Tamarack keeps I-9 forms and work authorization documents separate from other employee records. Only HR team members who handle work authorization can see them. Managers do not keep copies of employee documents.

## Tamarack does not give immigration legal advice

Tamarack HR handles the I-9 and reverification process only. Tamarack does not give immigration legal advice, such as advice about visa options, applications, status or timing. Employees with immigration legal questions should talk with an immigration attorney of their choice.

## Getting help with work authorization

Employees with questions about I-9 verification or reverification can contact HR at hr@tamarack-outdoor.example or open an immigration case in Tamarack HR Hub. The HR assistant in Tamarack HR Hub can open an immigration case for an employee. Immigration cases go to the HR Immigration and Compliance specialist, who contacts the employee within 2 business days.
