# Workplace Safety and Injury Reporting

This policy applies to all Tamarack Outdoor employees in stores, the Fort Worth Distribution Center, HQ and remote work locations. It covers how to stay safe at work and what to do after an injury. It is effective January 1, 2026.

## Emergencies at work

In a medical emergency, fire or other immediate danger at a Tamarack location, call 911 first. Then tell the manager on duty. Every store, the distribution center and HQ post an emergency exit map, the location of the first aid kit and the fire extinguishers near the time clock or main break area. Employees should know their location's evacuation meeting point, which is covered in safety training during the first week of work.

## Reporting a work injury

Any Tamarack employee injured at work, however minor the injury seems, must tell their manager or the manager on duty immediately. The employee must also submit an injury report in Tamarack HR Hub within 24 hours of the injury. If the employee cannot submit the report, their manager submits it for them. Near misses, where someone almost got hurt, should be reported the same way so the hazard can be fixed. Reporting an injury never counts against an employee.

## Getting medical care after a work injury

An employee injured at work should get the care they need. For emergencies, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. For non-emergency injuries, the manager helps the employee find a nearby clinic. Employees should tell the clinic the injury happened at work and keep copies of any paperwork. Medical details from the visit go to HR, not to the employee's manager.

## Workers' compensation at Tamarack

Tamarack carries workers' compensation insurance for work-related injuries and illnesses. After an injury report is submitted in Tamarack HR Hub, HR opens a safety case, contacts the employee and helps them file a claim with Tamarack's insurance carrier. The carrier decides what is covered. Employees who have questions about a claim can contact HR at hr@tamarack-outdoor.example.

## Store safety: ladders and lifting

Tamarack store employees must use the store's rolling ladders or step stools to reach high shelves and never climb shelving, fixtures or boxes. Keep three points of contact on a ladder and do not carry heavy items up it. For lifting, bend at the knees and keep the load close. Any item over 50 pounds, such as kayaks, packed tents or bike boxes, is a team lift: get a coworker or use a cart. Keep aisles and stockroom walkways clear and clean up spills right away.

## Distribution center safety

At the Fort Worth Distribution Center, employees must wear safety shoes and a high-visibility vest on the warehouse floor. Only employees who have completed Tamarack forklift certification may operate forklifts or pallet jacks with power. Walk only in marked pedestrian lanes. The team lift rule for items over 50 pounds also applies at the distribution center.

## Reporting a safety hazard

Employees who see a safety hazard, such as a broken ladder, a blocked exit or a wet floor, should fix it if they can do so safely and tell their manager. Hazards that are not fixed quickly can be reported through a safety case in Tamarack HR Hub or the ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142. Tamarack prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a hazard or an injury.
